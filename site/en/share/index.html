Your computer, shared with whoever you choose.

KyberNow Share lets other people onto your computer: friends who watch or play with you, someone close who borrows your gaming PC while you are not using it, a team taking turns on a workstation. They come in with a code, at home or from afar, with no account to create. You decide what each of them can do, and you take back control whenever you like.

Play together

Up to four guests on your screen.

You are playing or working, and you open a share: a friend watches your game from their phone, a second player picks up a controller from the sofa, a colleague follows your screen from their desk. You carry on using your computer as before.

You stay in charge

A guest arrives as a spectator: they see your screen and hear the sound, and send nothing to your computer. You give them their controllers, or the keyboard and mouse, or both, and take them back in one move. The Station only lets through what you have given, even if their device tried to send something else.

Their controller becomes the next controller

When you give a guest their controllers, your computer sees them as one more controller, plugged in after yours. Games for several players on the same screen work with no setting to change, as if your friend were sitting next to you.

A picture for each guest

Each guest picks the quality of their picture: Auto, the same as yours, 4K at 60 Hz, 1080p at 60 Hz or 720p at 30 Hz. It is never bigger than yours, and nothing they do changes your screen or your picture. A guest whose connection weakens gets a lighter picture, without you noticing.

Lend the computer

A powerful computer that serves several people.

A gaming computer or a workstation costs a lot, and it spends a good part of the day switched off or sitting idle. With KyberNow Share, you lend it while you are not using it: the person plays or works from their own device, at your place or from theirs, and you take it back whenever you like.

At home

Your games, and theirs, for family and friends

One good graphics card in a single machine is enough for the whole household. Your son plays on the living-room TV while you are at work, your friend picks up her game from her laptop, your brother borrows your computer from his place on a Saturday afternoon.

You choose what they can open: your game launchers, Steam, Epic, GOG, Xbox or Playnite, one game in particular, an emulator, any installed app. With Steam, you also decide at each loan whether the person plays with their own account, with their games and yours through Family Sharing, or with yours, with your library and your saves.

At work

One workstation for the whole team

The machine that carries the professional graphics card, the heavy software and the licences stays in the workshop. Your colleagues borrow it one after another, from a light laptop, a tablet or a plain browser, in the office or working from home.

You allow the software they need, Blender, DaVinci Resolve, a CAD or computation program, and you create an account per person, with time slots if you like: Tuesday morning for one, Thursday for another. Outside those, each of them asks, and you accept with one click.

Three ways to lend

A code for one session

“Lend the computer…”, from Admin, the icon next to the clock or your KyberNow Link app. You choose the length (1, 2 or 4 hours, or until you take it back), and you hand over the code, the link or the QR code. It works once.

Accounts for the people close to you

In Admin, you create an account per person: a first name, a username, a password. They receive an invitation link, and “Matthieu's computer” appears on the home screen of their app. When the computer is free, they borrow it without asking you.

Time slots

On a person's account, you set aside parts of the week: Wednesday from 2 PM to 6 PM, for example. During their slot, they go before you: if you are on the computer, you are warned two minutes ahead.

Your files are protected as well as we can, not watertight: a game or an allowed app can still read your disk. Lend your computer to people you trust.

Away from home

Your guests can be anywhere.

The same code, the same link and the same QR code work from anywhere. A guest who is not at your place goes through KyberNow's relay, a server on the internet open to everyone, and you accept each person who comes from afar before they get in. Nothing to open on your router.

Questions

The questions we get asked about Share.

Do I need to install anything extra?

No. KyberNow Share comes with KyberNow Station and the KyberNow Link apps. A guest who doesn't have the app simply opens the link in their browser.

Do my guests need an account?

No. For a share, a guest only gives their first name. To borrow the computer, they enter a code you give them, or the username and password of the account you created for them in Admin. Nothing is created online.

Can I carry on playing while guests watch?

Yes, that is the whole point of a share: you always come first. Your guests get their own picture, never bigger than yours, and nothing they do changes your screen. Lending, on the other hand, happens while you are not using the computer.

Can a guest see my files?

A spectator only sees your screen. A guest you give the keyboard and mouse to can do what you would do with them, while you watch. The person borrowing the computer only sees the apps you have allowed, but a game can read your disk: lend to people you trust.

How many people at once?

Four guests at most in a share. One loan at a time: during a loan, nobody else connects to the computer, and you yourself take back control with two minutes' notice.

What about a shared Mac?

A Mac with KyberNow Station takes guests like a PC: the same code, the same rights, the same relay.

What happens if my guest's connection drops?

A guest in a share comes back with the same code as long as you haven't asked for a new one. The person borrowing the computer can come back within fifteen minutes: their game is waiting for them.

Getting started

Share is already in KyberNow.

Install KyberNow Station on the computer you want to share and KyberNow Link on your devices: “Share” is in the session bar, “Lend the computer…” in Admin and next to the clock, “Join with a code” on the home screen of every app.
