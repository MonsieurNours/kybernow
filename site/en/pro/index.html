To keep the stream light, video keeps the brightness of every pixel but only one colour for every four (4:2:0). You won't notice on a film. On coloured code, an interface or a fine line, the edges bleed.

What comes with you

Your whole setup, from pen to webcam

Colour and HDR

Pictures in 10-bit, BT.2020 and PQ on an HDR screen, with test patterns to calibrate yours.

Pen

On an iPad, an Android tablet or a graphics tablet: pressure, tilt, rotation, eraser and button reach your software as if the tablet were plugged into the workstation.

No ceiling

Resolution and refresh rate depend only on the encoder in the workstation's graphics card, for example 5K at 60 Hz or 4K at 160 Hz.

All your screens, several machines

Each screen in front of you shows one of the workstation's screens. You can also open the render workstation and the editing Mac side by side.

Keyboard and clipboard

Every shortcut goes to the workstation, in full screen or all the time. Copy and paste works both ways.

Microphone and webcam

Your microphone and camera work in video calls started on the workstation.

Teams

Who can access what, in one place

  1. Sort machines into groups“Studio workstations”, “Render workstations”: a computer can belong to several groups.
  2. Open a group to your designersRights given to the group apply to its computers, today's and tomorrow's.
  3. Invite a freelancer with a linkA single-use link, valid for 7 days, with access until 31 December.
  4. Everything goes in the logEvery connection, every link, every change of rights, with the time and where it came from.
