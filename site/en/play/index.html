For players

The usual remote-play problems, sorted in advance

  • Your controllers, recognised

    Your device's controller becomes an Xbox controller on the PC. Up to four players at once.

  • Your games' HDR

    HDR games keep their colour and contrast. Test patterns help you set up your screen.

  • < 1 mscompression and decompression

    Even less delay over a cable

    On a wired network, the app switches to PyroWave, a picture format that compresses in a fraction of a millisecond.

  • The picture holds up when the Wi-Fi wobbles

    A damaged frame is repaired on arrival. When the network is saturated, the app lowers the bitrate, then raises it again.

  • Your game launcher opens by itself

    Steam Big Picture opens on the TV, Playnite on the Steam Deck, and everything closes at the end of the session.

  • Sound on your speakers

    Stereo, 5.1 or 7.1 depending on the device, right through to your soundbar or amplifier.
