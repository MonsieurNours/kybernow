Play on the TV with Apple TV

On Apple TV, your computer fills the TV screen, up to 4K at 60 Hz, with four controllers, 5.1 or 7.1 sound and the Siri Remote as a mouse. Ideally, you add the computer without typing anything on the remote.

Before you start

  • An Apple TV 4K from 2021 or later, running tvOS 18 or later.
  • The Station installed on the computer you want to reach.
  • A Bluetooth controller for gaming: Xbox, DualSense, DualShock or MFi. Pair it in the Apple TV's Settings › Remotes and Devices.

Install the app

  1. On the Apple TV, open the App Store and search for “KyberNow”.
  2. Install the app, then open KyberNow from the Home Screen.
  3. Allow access to the local network: without it, the app cannot find your computer.

The first time, three welcome screens offer you a KyberNow account. On the TV, you sign in with a code: a QR code and a code appear, which you approve from your phone (or at share.kybernow.app/tv). “Continue without an account” skips this step. See Your KyberNow account.

Add your computer

Choose “Add a computer or a console”, then one of these ways:

  • From your Mac (recommended): the computers already added in KyberNow Link on your Mac arrive with their access, through iCloud Keychain. Nothing to type, and a password changed on the Mac follows by itself. The Mac and the Apple TV need the same iCloud account.
  • On your network: your computer shows up by itself. Enter the account created when the Station was installed. A notification appears on your iPhone: type with its keyboard, or fill it in with one gesture from your Keychain.
  • On-screen code: the Apple TV shows a six-digit code. Accept it in Admin, in the Link devices space (on a PC, also in the panel of the icon by the clock). Nothing to type. This code only travels over the local network.
  • Address: the computer's IP address or name and Admin's port, then the account. This is the one to choose over a VPN. Compare the fingerprint shown with the one in Admin, under Link devices › “Add a device”.

“With a friend account” is for borrowing a computer, “PlayStation” for playing your console.

Then choose a profile (Game, Desktop, Max or Auto), and “Connect”.

On the Apple TV Home Screen

Put KyberNow in the top row of the Apple TV Home Screen. The “Resume on a computer” row then shows each computer with its last picture: one click opens the session directly.

Each member of the household has their own computers and settings: the app follows the user chosen in the Apple TV's Control Centre.

With the remote and the controller

  • With the menu closed, the controller and the remote only talk to the game.
  • The remote's Back button, or L1 + R1 + Start + Select held together, opens the session menu: statistics, profile, “Remote as a mouse”, controller as a mouse and “Disconnect”.
  • With “Remote as a mouse”, the Siri Remote's touch surface moves the pointer, a click clicks and Play/Pause right-clicks. In the Desktop profile, it is on from the start.
  • A Bluetooth keyboard and mouse work too.

The details are in Keyboard, mouse and shortcuts.

Recommended settings

  • Picture: the “Match this display” resolution, up to 4K at 60 Hz, the Apple TV's limit. Let the app set the TV to the session's refresh rate.
  • HDR: on an HDR TV, turn on “Match Dynamic Range” in the Apple TV's video settings.
  • Sound: Auto follows the Apple TV's output, in stereo, 5.1 or 7.1 to an HDMI amplifier. In AirPods, the sound switches to spatial audio. The app warns you when an output adds delay (AirPods, HomePod, AirPlay).
  • Network: an Ethernet cable on the Apple TV, or 5 GHz or 6 GHz Wi-Fi close to the router. PyroWave needs an Apple TV 4K from 2022 and a cable.

If something goes wrong

  • The computer does not show up. It must be on, on the same network, with KyberNow Station running. Also check that KyberNow has access to the local network, in the Apple TV's settings. Over a VPN, use Address.
  • “No computer published by your Mac yet”. Open KyberNow Link on the Mac, with the same iCloud account.
  • “Username or password rejected”. Type the Station's account again, the one chosen during its installation or created in Admin › Users.
  • “The Station's certificate does not match its announcement”. It may not be your computer. Compare the fingerprint with the one in Admin.
  • The sound arrives late. Use the Apple TV's HDMI output rather than AirPlay or Bluetooth headphones.
