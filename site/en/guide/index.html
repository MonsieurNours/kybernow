Guide and FAQ

Installing KyberNow, step by step.

Three minutes on the computer you share, one on each device. This guide follows the screens in order, then answers frequently asked questions.

Before you start

The computer you share

  • Windows 10 or 11, with administrator rights
  • Any graphics card, NVIDIA, AMD or Intel, eGPUs included. Without a graphics card, the processor compresses the image
  • Microsoft Edge WebView2, present on Windows 11 and most Windows 10 installs (the installer guides you if it is missing)
  • Steam, if you want the sound on your devices rather than on the computer's speakers

Your devices

  • A Mac with Apple silicon on macOS 14 or later, for the app
  • A PC on Windows 10 22H2 or Windows 11, x64 or ARM64 (Snapdragon, on Windows 11), for the app
  • An Apple TV 4K from 2021 or later on tvOS 18 or later, and a Mac with Xcode to install the app on it
  • An iPhone or iPad on iOS 18 or iPadOS 18 or later, and a Mac with Xcode to install the app on it
  • A phone, tablet or TV on Android 11 or later (Android TV, Google TV, NVIDIA Shield)
  • Or any recent browser, on a computer, tablet or phone
  • On the same network as the computer, or linked to it by a VPN

An Ethernet cable on the computer avoids Wi-Fi micro-dropouts. On the device side, 5 GHz or 6 GHz Wi-Fi close to the router is usually enough.

Step 1

Install KyberNow Station

Download KyberNow-Station-Setup.exe on the computer you want to share and open it. Windows asks for administrator permission: the installer adds drivers and services. Nothing changes on the computer until you click “Install”.

  1. Welcome

    The installer checks the computer while you read: graphics card and driver, network, ports, main display, power mode, remote sound. An orange dot explains what it recommends, without blocking you.

    Two ports are picked at random, one for the stream, the other for KyberNow Admin, avoiding those used by Windows and Sunshine. “Change” lets you choose them. A network set to “Public” will switch to “Private”, otherwise your devices would not see the computer.

  2. Your account

    A username and a password of at least 10 characters. It is KyberNow's only account: it opens Admin and connects your devices. It is stored only as a hash.

  3. Options

    Everything is already set for you: a virtual display matched to each device, gamepads, remote sound, starting with the computer, maximum performance. The virtual display driver (SudoVDA, open source) is not signed by Microsoft: its certificate is added to the computer's trusted authorities, and removed when you uninstall.

  4. Installation, then Done

    Less than a minute. The final screen shows the computer's address, a QR code for Admin and the address of Link in the browser. If the gamepads require it, a restart is offered.

The first time you open Admin, the browser reports an unknown certificate: this is expected, the Station created its own certificate. The KyberNow Link apps, for their part, verify its fingerprint.

A KyberNow Station icon appears in the notification area. It shows the state of the Station (green when running, purple during a session) and opens Admin, where you will find five spaces: Overview, Picture and sound, Link devices, Users and System.

Step 2

Connect a device

Pick your device: its installation shows up just below.

MacSigned and notarised Windowsx64 and ARM64 PCs Apple TVFrom a Mac iPhone and iPadFrom a Mac AndroidAndroid 11 or later BrowserNothing to install

With the Mac app

  1. Download KyberNow-Link-macOS.zip, open it and drag the app into Applications. The app is signed and notarised by Apple.

  2. Open it, then choose “Add a computer”:

    • On this network: the computer shows up on its own (Bonjour).
    • By address: its IP address or name, for example over a VPN.
    • QR code or link: in Admin, on the Link devices page, “Add a device”. The link contains the address and the certificate fingerprint, never a password.

  3. “Is this your computer?”: when it comes from a link or from Bonjour, the fingerprint is verified automatically. With a plain address, compare the first four groups with those shown in Admin.

  4. Enter the account created during installation. It is kept in the Mac's Keychain. Click the computer: the session opens.

With the Windows app

For a laptop or a modest PC that connects to a more powerful one. The image is decoded by the graphics card (H.264, HEVC, and AV1 when it can), displayed with Direct3D 11: about 10 ms of latency measured on a home network.

  1. Download KyberNow-Link-Setup.exe and open it. A single installer for x64 and ARM64 PCs: it installs the right version by itself. The app is installed for you only, with no administrator rights.

  2. Open KyberNow Link, then choose “Add a computer”:

    • On the network: the computer shows up on its own.
    • With the link: in Admin, on the Link devices page, “Add a device”. The kybernow:// link opens the app with the computer and its fingerprint already verified.
    • By address: its IP address or name, for example over a VPN. Compare the first four groups of the fingerprint with those shown in Admin.

  3. To get access, accept the six-digit code shown by the app in Admin, on the Link devices page: nothing to type. Or enter the account created during installation, kept in Windows Credential Manager. Click the computer: the session opens.

During the beta, the app is not signed with Authenticode yet: on first launch, Windows SmartScreen shows a warning. Click “More info”, then “Run anyway”.

Your computers also appear when you right-click the KyberNow Link icon in the taskbar: one click starts the session.

On Apple TV

Until it is published on the App Store, the app is installed from a Mac, signed with your own Apple account. Allow ten minutes the first time, one minute after that. You need an Apple TV 4K from 2021 or later, up to date, and a Mac on the same network with Xcode (free on the Mac App Store; open it once so that it finishes installing).

  1. On the Mac, install XcodeGen with Homebrew, then get the KyberNow code:

    brew install xcodegen
git clone https://github.com/MonsieurNours/kybernow.git
cd kybernow

  2. In Xcode › Settings › Accounts, add your Apple account. Note its team identifier, ten characters such as ABCDE12345: Xcode shows it when you choose the team that signs an app.

  3. On the Apple TV, open Settings › Remotes and Devices › Remote App and Devices. On the Mac, in Xcode, open Window › Devices and Simulators, choose the Apple TV, click Pair and type the code shown on the TV.

  4. With the Apple TV awake, run the installation from the kybernow folder:

    apps/tvos/scripts/sideload.sh --team ABCDE12345

    With a paid account (Apple Developer Program), add --full: computers added from the Mac through iCloud, Top Shelf and per-user profiles on the Apple TV.

  5. Open KyberNow on the Apple TV, allow local network access, then add the computer:

    • From your Mac: the Mac app's computers arrive through iCloud, with their credentials. Only when installed with --full.
    • On the network: the computer shows up on its own; type the account, with the iPhone keyboard if you like.
    • Code on screen: accept it in Admin, on the Link devices page.
    • Address: its IP address or name, for example over a VPN.

With a free Apple account, the app stops opening after 7 days: run the same command again to renew it, and your computers and settings stay. With a paid account, it lasts a year.

Full procedure and troubleshooting: installing KyberNow Link on your Apple TV.

On iPhone and iPad

Until TestFlight and the App Store, the app is installed from a Mac, signed with your own Apple account, as for the Apple TV. You need an iPhone or iPad on iOS 18 or iPadOS 18 or later, a cable to connect it to the Mac the first time, and Xcode on the Mac.

  1. On the Mac, install XcodeGen with Homebrew, then get the KyberNow code:

    brew install xcodegen
git clone https://github.com/MonsieurNours/kybernow.git
cd kybernow

  2. In Xcode › Settings › Accounts, add your Apple account and note its team identifier (ten characters such as ABCDE12345).

  3. Connect the iPhone or iPad, unlock it and accept Trust This Computer. Turn on Developer Mode: Settings › Privacy & Security › Developer Mode (the device restarts).

  4. Run the installation from the kybernow folder:

    apps/ios/scripts/sideload.sh --team ABCDE12345

    With a paid account (Apple Developer Program), add --full: computers added from the Mac through iCloud and the shared Keychain.

  5. With a free account, first allow the app: Settings › General › VPN & Device Management, your account, Trust. Open KyberNow, allow local network access, then add the computer:

    • From your Mac: the Mac app's computers arrive through iCloud, with their credentials. Only when installed with --full.
    • On the network: the computer shows up on its own; type its account.
    • Code or QR code: shown by Admin, on the Link devices page.
    • Address: its IP address or name, for example over a VPN.

With a free Apple account, the app stops opening after 7 days: run the same command again to renew it. The SDK the script downloads is attached to releases from 0.4.0 on.

Full procedure and troubleshooting: installing KyberNow Link on your iPhone or iPad.

On Android

A single app for phones, tablets and TVs (Android TV, Google TV, NVIDIA Shield), from Android 11. On the TV: up to 4K at 60 Hz, 7.1 sound on an HDMI receiver, Bluetooth gamepads and the remote. Until it is on the Play Store, the app is installed from its file, KyberNow-Link-Android.apk.

On a phone or tablet

  1. On the device, open the KyberNow-Link-Android.apk link, then the downloaded file.

  2. Android asks you to allow your browser to install apps: allow it, go back, then tap Install.

On a TV: the simplest way is through a computer on the same network, with adb, Android's developer tool. Allow five minutes the first time.

  1. On the TV, turn on developer options: Settings › Device Preferences › About (on Google TV: Settings › System › About), then click Build seven times. In Developer options, turn on Network debugging (or ADB debugging) and note the TV's address.

  2. On the computer, download KyberNow-Link-Android.apk and install adb: on a Mac with Homebrew, brew install android-platform-tools; elsewhere, Google's SDK Platform-Tools.

  3. Connect the computer to the TV, then install the app, from the folder of the downloaded file:

    adb connect 192.168.1.30
adb install KyberNow-Link-Android.apk

    Replace the address with the TV's. On the first adb connect, it asks you to allow debugging: tick “Always allow” and confirm. You can turn debugging off afterwards.

Then, everywhere: open KyberNow and add the computer.

  • On the network: the computer shows up on its own.
  • Code on screen: accept the six-digit code shown by the app in Admin, on the Link devices page: nothing to type.
  • QR code, on a phone or tablet: Admin's, on the Link devices page, “Add a device”.
  • Address: its IP address or name, for example over a VPN.

In a browser

On any device on the same network (or linked by VPN), open Admin's address followed by /ecran, for example https://192.168.1.20:31415/ecran. The exact address is shown at the end of the installation and on Admin's Overview page. Accept the certificate warning, then sign in with your account.

Link in the browser is good for troubleshooting, or for working from a tablet or a borrowed computer. For gaming, the native app decodes the image faster.

Step 3

In a session

When you connect, the computer creates a virtual display at your device's resolution and refresh rate, turns off its own displays and sends the sound to your speakers. At the end, everything goes back to how it was: displays, audio output. Windows is not locked.

The profiles

GameLowest latency, your screen's refresh rate, gamepad ready.
DesktopSharp text (4:4:4), no dropped images, higher bitrate.
MaxNo compromise: lowest latency, 4:4:4 and a high bitrate. Ideal on a wired network.

Changing a setting switches the profile to “Custom”. The mouse follows Auto mode: desktop mode normally, game mode (raw movement) when a game goes full screen on the computer.

Mac app shortcuts

⌃⌥⌘DDisconnect
⌃⌥⌘FFull screen or window
⌃⌥⌘MMouse in game or desktop mode
⌃⌥⌘SDetailed measurements: path of an image, latency, bitrate, losses
L1R1StartSelectThe gamepad controls the session bar

⌃⌥⌘ shortcuts stay on the Mac and are never sent to the computer. ⌘ + key is sent as Ctrl + key.

Windows app shortcuts

CtrlAltShiftDDisconnect
CtrlAltShiftFFull screen or window
CtrlAltShiftMMouse in game or desktop mode

The session bar appears when the pointer touches the top of the screen. In full screen, Windows shortcuts, including the ⊞ key and AltTab, are sent to the computer; the Keys menu sends CtrlAltDel and the other reserved combinations. A session shows on a single screen for now.

On Apple TV

BackOpens the session menu: stream health, gamepads, sound, remote as a mouse, text entry, disconnect
L1R1StartSelectHeld together, open the same menu from the gamepad
Touch surfaceAs a mouse: your finger moves the pointer, a click is a left click, Play/Pause a right click

Up to four gamepads (Xbox, DualSense, DualShock), each one a player on the computer, with rumble. A Bluetooth keyboard and mouse work too.

On an Android TV

BackOpens the session menu: stream health, profiles, sound, remote or gamepad as mouse, text input, keys, measurements, disconnect
L1R1StartSelectHeld together, open the same menu from the gamepad
Gamepad as mouseLeft stick: the pointer. A click, X right click, right stick wheel, Start the ⊞ key, Select Esc, L1 R1 Alt + Tab

In the menu, the gamepad drives the app: A selects, B closes. “Disconnect”, in red, is the last tile.

Away from home

KyberNow goes through no third-party server: to reach the computer from afar, link your devices to it with a VPN. Tailscale is the simplest, WireGuard the lightest.

  1. Install the same VPN on the computer and on the device, signed in to the same account or joined to the same network.

  2. In the app, add the computer “By address”, using its VPN address (for example 100.x.y.z with Tailscale), or scan the QR code from Admin.

  3. Choose the Game or Desktop profile depending on your connection: the Max profile's bitrate is designed for a local network.

Do not open KyberNow's ports to the Internet on your router: the VPN keeps the computer invisible to the rest of the world.

Updating

The Station: Admin checks for new versions every 6 hours. In System › Updates, “Install now” downloads the package, verifies its fingerprint and applies it in about a minute (a session in progress is ended). The icon in the notification area offers it too.

The Mac app: it updates itself, or through KyberNow Link › Check for Updates…

The Windows app: it updates itself, with signed updates. Its settings (the gear in the title bar) also offer beta versions.

The Apple TV app: in the kybernow folder, run git pull, then the installation command again. With a free Apple account, do it before the 7 days are up.

The Android app: it checks for new versions and offers to install them, with signed updates; its settings (App settings › Updates) offer beta versions too. On a TV, the first time, Android asks you to allow KyberNow to install apps.

Without Admin, from PowerShell as administrator:

irm https://github.com/MonsieurNours/kybernow/releases/latest/download/update.ps1 | iex

Uninstalling

The Station: Windows Settings › Apps › Installed apps › KyberNow Station › Uninstall. Services, Admin, the virtual display and its certificate, firewall rules and settings are removed. The ViGEmBus gamepad driver stays installed, because other software uses it.

The Mac: drag KyberNow Link from Applications to the Trash.

The Windows PC: Windows Settings › Apps › Installed apps › KyberNow Link › Uninstall. The app offers to erase your computers, settings and passwords as well.

The Apple TV: on the Home Screen, press and hold the touch surface on the KyberNow icon, then choose to delete the app.

Android: Settings › Apps, KyberNow, then Uninstall.

Frequently asked questions

Is KyberNow really free?

Yes. It is a community, non-commercial project under the AGPL-3.0 free software licence: no online account, no subscription, no ads. The code is on GitHub.

Why “beta”?

KyberNow has not reached version 1.0 yet. The 0.x versions are already fine for everyday use, but some bugs remain, and settings and features may still change from one version to the next. Keep the Station and your apps up to date, and report problems on GitHub or on Discord.

Can KyberNow replace Sunshine and Moonlight?

Yes: your computer on all your devices, in the app or in the browser. KyberNow relies on the Kyber engine and on QUIC rather than on the GameStream protocol: the image is always encrypted, a single port to open, a built-in virtual display at the device's resolution, and pairing with no PIN to copy. It still lacks HDR: see the comparison.

Is KyberNow cloud gaming?

It is your own cloud. Instead of renting a computer in a data centre, KyberNow makes yours reachable from your other devices, at home or from afar through a VPN. No subscription and no queue: your games and software as they are installed, and the image goes through no third-party server.

Can I play my PC games on a Mac or an Apple TV?

Yes: the game runs on the Windows computer, with its graphics card, and the image arrives on the Mac or the Apple TV. Any game or launcher, with a gamepad (Xbox, PlayStation), a keyboard and a mouse. The Mac app follows your screen's refresh rate, 120 Hz or more; the Apple TV goes up to 4K at 60 Hz.

Do I need a specific graphics card?

No. Kyber encodes the image with any graphics card, NVIDIA, AMD or Intel, including an external one (eGPU). Without a graphics card, the processor does the job. The encoder is chosen in KyberNow Admin, on the System › Advanced page.

Which resolutions and refresh rates?

Those of your screen: the computer creates a virtual display to match it, 4K, 5K or 6K, 60, 120 or 160 Hz. We have used it at 3840 × 2160 and 160 Hz, and at 5K and 60 Hz. The “4K at most” option lightens encoding if your network or your graphics card struggles.

What latency should I expect?

On a local network, we measure a median of 9.4 ms from capture to display, in 4K at 160 Hz. It depends mostly on the graphics card, the network and the device. The app shows it live in its session bar.

What about HDR?

Not yet: Kyber 0.29 captures the screen in 8 bits. Keep Windows HDR turned off during sessions.

My displays turn off during the session. Is that normal?

Yes: the computer only shows the virtual display, at the size of your device. They turn back on at the end. If in doubt, Admin offers “Turn my displays back on”, and the driver removes the virtual display on its own if it stops receiving a signal.

The sound still comes out of the computer's speakers.

Remote sound uses Steam's virtual speakers. Install Steam on the computer, then run the KyberNow Station installer again: the “Remote sound” option becomes available.

My gamepad does not respond.

Xbox and PlayStation gamepads go through the ViGEmBus driver, added during installation. Restarting the computer is sometimes needed after the first installation.

Can two devices connect at the same time?

No: the second one takes over, the first one is disconnected and the virtual display is recreated at the size of the new device.

The image stays black or freezes.

Update the Station and the app: the app asks for a full image again after a loss, and lightens the stream if the image does not arrive. Prefer an Ethernet cable on the computer, and check that no other streaming app is using the encoder.

I forgot the password.

On the computer, right-click the KyberNow Station icon › “Reset the Admin password…”, then follow Admin to choose a new one.

Does the app work on an Intel Mac?

No, it requires Apple silicon (M1 or later). On an Intel Mac, use KyberNow Link in the browser.

What about Android?

Yes: a single app for phones, tablets and TVs, from Android 11. Until it is on the Play Store, it is installed from its APK file: see On Android.

What about the iPhone and iPad app?

Not on the App Store or TestFlight yet. Until then, it is installed from a Mac with Xcode, signed with your own Apple account: see On iPhone and iPad. It needs iOS 18 or iPadOS 18 or later.

Why is the Apple TV app not on the App Store?

Not yet. Until then, it is installed from a Mac with Xcode, signed with your own Apple account: see On Apple TV. It needs an Apple TV 4K from 2021 or later; older models cannot run the app.

How is KyberNow related to Kyber?

KyberNow uses Kyber, the open-source streaming engine built by VLC developers, and adds KyberNow Station (installer, virtual displays and speakers), KyberNow Admin and the KyberNow Link apps. KyberNow is neither published nor endorsed by the Kyber team, and is not affiliated with it. Our fixes to the engine are published.

What data does KyberNow collect?

None. Nothing goes through a server of ours: the image goes directly from the computer to your devices. KyberNow Admin only asks GitHub whether a new version exists.
