Frequently asked questions

The questions people ask us

KyberNow shows your computer on your other screens and lets you control it, for playing as well as working. You install KyberNow Station on the computer, then the KyberNow Link app on your devices. KyberNow Admin, in the browser, sets up the Station, and KyberNow Share lets someone else in. See What you need to get started.

Yes. KyberNow is free, open-source software under the AGPL-3.0 licence, developed by Les Lignes Claires with no commercial aim. There is no subscription and no advertising, and the public relay is open to everyone. The KyberNow account, families and teams are free too. The code is on GitHub.

KyberNow hasn't reached version 1.0 yet. We use it every day, but settings or screens may still change from one version to the next. Keep the Station and your apps up to date (see Update KyberNow), and report problems on GitHub or Discord.

For many uses, yes. Like them, KyberNow shows your computer on your other devices, in an app or in a browser. It is built on the Kyber engine rather than the GameStream protocol: the picture is always encrypted, the virtual display at the device's size is included, and most devices are added by accepting a code in Admin, with nothing to copy out. The Technology page compares the two in detail.
