Your own cloud, at home

All the power of your computer, on each of your devices.

Cloud gaming rents you a computer hundreds of kilometres away. KyberNow does the opposite: it makes your computer reachable from your other devices, on your network or through your VPN. Your graphics card, your software, your files, your games. No third-party server between you and it.

Play

Gamepad, relative mouse, your screen's refresh rate: 120, 144 Hz or more. Game mode turns on by itself when a game goes full screen.

Work

A remote desktop with sharp text in 4:4:4, shared clipboard, the exact resolution of your screen: 4K, 5K, 6K.

Everywhere

Apps for Mac, Windows, Apple TV, iPhone and iPad, and any browser: computer, tablet, phone. At home, or from afar through your VPN.

The transport

QUIC: four lanes, none waits for the others.

Kyber carries the image, the sound and your input over QUIC, the HTTP/3 protocol standardised by the IETF (RFC 9000). Where a single stream stops at the first lost packet, QUIC keeps each lane independent and repairs losses on arrival.

Encrypted by default

TLS 1.3 is part of QUIC: the image and your keystrokes never travel in the clear, even at home.

Independent lanes

No head-of-line blocking: a lost image packet delays neither the sound nor the gamepad.

Losses repaired

Error correction on arrival; beyond that, the app immediately asks for a full image.

One port

TCP and UDP on the same number: one firewall rule, one path through the VPN.

The foundation

Built on Kyber.

KyberNow uses Kyber, a free, open-source streaming engine (AGPL-3.0) built by VLC developers. Written in Rust, relying on FFmpeg and QUIC, it aims for a few milliseconds of latency. KyberNow turns it into a complete product, ready to install.

What Kyber does

  • Capture and encoding, on any graphics card or the processor
  • QUIC transport and error correction
  • Remote keyboard, mouse and gamepads
  • Development kit for clients

What KyberNow adds

  • KyberNow Station: installer, administration in the browser (KyberNow Admin) and updates
  • Virtual display and speakers for each session
  • KyberNow Link: native apps for Mac, Windows, Apple TV, iPhone and iPad (decoding and display by the app), and its web version
  • Engine fixes, published and documented

A community, non-commercial project

KyberNow is free and open source: no account, no subscription, no ads, and all the code is open. It is developed by Les Lignes Claires. KyberNow is not affiliated with Kyber: it is neither published nor endorsed by the Kyber team.

In detail

Thought through to the last pixel.

Your screen, to the pixel

With each session, the computer creates a virtual display at your device's resolution and refresh rate (4K, 5K, 6K, 120 Hz and more), turns off its own displays, then turns them back on at the end. Nothing to set up.

2560 × 14403840 × 21605120 × 2880

HD sound, up to 7.1

A virtual output in place of the computer's speakers, 10 ms packets, about twenty milliseconds of buffer.

Variable refresh rate

Each image is shown as soon as it is decoded: on a variable refresh rate screen, the screen follows the stream.

Pairing at a glance

The computer shows up on its own on your network. Otherwise, use a QR code or a link from KyberNow Admin, and its fingerprint is verified.

Nothing to install

The Station also serves a web version of Link: open its address in a browser, on a computer, tablet or phone, with a trackpad or a finger.

One-click updates

From Admin for the Station, from the app for your devices. Each package is verified before it is installed.

Three profiles

Lowest latency

The highest refresh rate your screen can show, relative mouse, gamepad. 4:2:0 colour to save every millisecond.
