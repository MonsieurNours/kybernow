Everyday use

Your PC follows you around the house, and beyond.

KyberNow Link runs on your devices and shows your PC's picture, with its sound. Your games and software stay on the PC: nothing to reinstall elsewhere, and your games pick up right where you left them.

In the living room

Game night on the TV

Your PC games on the big screen, with up to four controllers at once. On the TV, everything works with a controller or the remote, and the sound goes to your soundbar or amplifier.

  • Android TV and Google TV
  • LG TVs
  • Raspberry Pi
  • SteamOS in the living room

On the sofa

On a tablet, a phone or a Steam Deck

Carry on with your game in comfort while the TV is busy with something else. Touch controls on tablets and phones, a controller on the Steam Deck.

  • iPhone and iPad
  • Android
  • Steam Deck

At the desk

Your PC, from another computer

The powerful PC stays in its room while you work from your Mac or a light laptop, with keyboard, mouse and copy-paste. On a borrowed computer, a browser is enough.

  • Mac
  • Windows
  • Linux
  • Browser

Away from home

At a hotel or at a friend's

Left your PC at home? With a VPN such as Tailscale or WireGuard, you can reach it wherever you are, games and files alike. It just needs to stay switched on.

  • With a VPN

Before you install

What you need to get started.

A PC running Windows 10 or 11

The one that runs your games and software. Any NVIDIA, AMD or Intel graphics card will do, and without one the processor takes over. Install Steam on it so the sound follows you to your devices.

A home network

The PC and your devices on the same network, over Wi-Fi or cable. An Ethernet cable on the PC gives the steadiest picture.

Your devices

A Mac, another Windows or Linux PC, an Apple TV, an iPhone, an iPad, an Android device, an LG TV, a Steam Deck, a Raspberry Pi, or simply a web browser.

A VPN, to go beyond your home

Optional. To play or work away from home, a VPN such as Tailscale or WireGuard links your devices to your PC.

No online account to create and no subscription: you just choose a name and a password when you install.

On your devices

The app that shows your PC full screen on your devices, with sound, controller, keyboard and mouse. On a device without the app, a browser is enough.

Ready out of the box

Remote play hassles, sorted in advance.

Elsewhere you often have to add a display driver, set up the controller and launch your games by hand. KyberNow takes care of all that, with no extra add-ons to install.

A picture sized to every screen

Each time you connect, the PC creates a virtual screen at the device's resolution and refresh rate, turns off its own screens and turns them back on at the end. Your Mac gets its Retina resolution at 120 Hz, the TV its 4K. We have checked it up to 4K at 160 Hz.

1920 × 1080 · 60 Hz2560 × 1600 · 120 Hz3840 × 2160 · 160 Hz

Under 10 ms of delay

On a local network, a picture takes 9.4 milliseconds to travel from your PC to your screen (measured median), less than a hundredth of a second. KyberNow is built on Kyber, an engine designed for speed by developers from the VLC project.

It holds up when the Wi-Fi wobbles

A picture damaged on the way is repaired on arrival or requested again straight away. If the connection drops for a few seconds, the app reconnects on its own and the game carries on.

Your controllers, recognised by the PC

Your device's controller becomes an Xbox controller for the PC, and games recognise it without any setup. Up to four players at once.

Sound on your speakers

The PC's sound comes out of your device, in stereo, 5.1 or 7.1 depending on what it supports, all the way to your soundbar or amplifier. Steam just needs to be installed on the PC.

Encrypted, with no online account

The picture and your keystrokes travel encrypted, even at home, straight from the PC to your device with no third-party server. A device can only connect once you have approved it in Admin.

Your PC becomes a console

Pick in Admin the app that opens at the start of each session, device by device: Steam Big Picture for the TV, Playnite for the Steam Deck, nothing for the Mac. It closes at the end. Steam, Playnite, Xbox, Epic, GOG and others are detected automatically.

What makes KyberNow so responsive.

9.4 msThe trip of a picture, from the graphics card to your screen, measured step by step. QUICThe protocol that carries picture, sound and controls, encrypted, on separate channels, so a loss on one does not hold up the others. KyberThe open-source engine, written in Rust by developers from the VLC project, that KyberNow is built on.

Questions

The questions we hear most.

Is it really free?

Yes. KyberNow is a community, non-commercial project under the open-source AGPL licence. No subscription, no ads, no online account.

Which devices can I use?

A Mac, a Windows or Linux PC, an Apple TV, an iPhone, an iPad, an Android device (phone, tablet, Android TV), a recent LG TV, a Steam Deck or a Raspberry Pi, and anything else through a web browser. The PC you share must run Windows.

Do I need a fast internet connection?

Not at home: the picture travels over your local network without going out to the internet. Recent Wi-Fi is enough, and an Ethernet cable on the PC gives the steadiest picture. Away from home, quality depends on both connections, the one at home and the one where you are.

Is it safe?

Everything between the PC and your devices is encrypted, even at home. A device can only connect once you have approved it in Admin, and nothing goes through a server of ours.

Can I play away from home?

Yes, with a VPN such as Tailscale or WireGuard, which links your devices to your PC as if they were at home. The PC needs to stay switched on.

How is it different from Sunshine and Moonlight?

Both let you play remotely. KyberNow installs in one go, creates a screen the right size on its own, always encrypts the picture and also works in a browser. The details are on the Technology page.
