Out of the box

Everything, right away.

No other solution brings all of this together, out of the box, at this level of latency. With KyberNow it is the default: install, connect, nothing else.

4K at 120 Hz, 5K, 6K, 240 Hz: your screen's resolution and refresh rate

With each session, the computer creates a virtual display at exactly the size and refresh rate of the device that connects, turns off its own displays, then turns them back on at the end. The only limit is your graphics card's encoder.

2560 × 1440 · 240 Hz3840 × 2160 · 120 Hz6016 × 3384 · 60 Hz

7.1 sound, up to studio quality

Opus in stereo, 5.1 or 7.1 to your receiver, in 10 ms packets, up to the codec's maximum. The computer goes silent, the device listens.

Variable refresh rate

Each image is shown as soon as it is decoded: ProMotion, Adaptive-Sync, the TV's or the Deck's VRR lock onto the stream.

Shared clipboard, sharp text

Copy here, paste there: text travels both ways. Full 4:4:4 colour for text without smearing, on the devices that can decode it.

Every gamepad, rumble included

Xbox, PlayStation, Nintendo, Steam Deck, any gamepad your device recognises, rumble included. Game mode turns on by itself when a game goes full screen.

Encrypted, one port, no account

QUIC and TLS 1.3 always, one firewall rule, one path through the VPN. No third-party server, no sign-up.

Your computer becomes a console

For each device, choose in KyberNow Admin the app that opens on the computer as soon as the session starts: Steam Big Picture on the Apple TV, Playnite on the Deck, nothing on the Mac. It closes at the end. No path to type: the apps come from the Start menu, with their icons. With a Raspberry Pi running KyberNow Link OS, the console fits in your hand.

The gamepad is not just for playing: in every Link app it drives everything, the home screen, the profiles, the settings, the session menu. On the TV as on the Deck, the phone or the computer. Nothing to add, no third-party software.
