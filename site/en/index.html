How it works

From your computer to your screen, in six steps

9.4ms
  1. 01CaptureCopied from the computer's screen, real or virtualt = 0
  2. 02EncodingCompressed by the graphics card5.19 ms
  3. 03SendingSplit into encrypted packets0.12 ms
  4. 04NetworkCarried across the local network0.66 ms
  5. 05DecodingDecompressed by your device2.50 ms
  6. 06DisplayOn screen as soon as it is decoded0.62 ms

Measured on 19 October 2026 on a local network, from a PC with an RTX 3080 Ti to a MacBook Pro M4 Max. The whole trip and each step are medians. The details are on the Technology page.
