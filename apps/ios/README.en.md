# KyberNow Link for iPhone and iPad

English · [Français](README.md)

KyberNow Link, KyberNow's streaming client on iPhone and iPad: you get your computer (KyberNow Station: Kyber 0.29 server + KyberNow Admin) in full screen, by touch, with the iPad's keyboard and trackpad, or with a controller. v2 identity ([guidelines](../../brand/v2/README.en.md)); mock-ups: [Claude Design](https://claude.ai/artifact/4ef5m4JSRnzuitJcuCFjMX) (home, adding, transition, iPhone and iPad session, measurements, settings, exception states, icon, launch). Screens and wording shared by every KyberNow Link app: [docs/link-ui.en.md](../../docs/link-ui.en.md).

iOS and iPadOS 18 or later. Same identifier as the Mac and Apple TV apps (`fr.leslignesclaires.kybernow`: shared Keychain and iCloud). The app is neither on the App Store nor on TestFlight: **installing it from a Mac**: [SIDELOAD.en.md](SIDELOAD.en.md). The app rests on the Rust core, like the Mac and Apple TV apps ([docs/coeur-apple.en.md](../../docs/coeur-apple.en.md)).

## What the app does

- **Home**: the chosen computer full frame (its last image, a preview requested from Admin, otherwise its signature) and, in the order of [docs/link-ui.en.md](../../docs/link-ui.en.md), nothing else: its state (“Online”, “Offline”, “Searching…” until it has answered), its name, its system, the error that keeps you out if there is one (password refused, Station reinstalled), the display the session will ask for (“2622 × 1206 display at 120 Hz, sized for this screen”, or “The computer's own displays, as they are”), “Connect” (when offline: “Wake the computer” when the app knows one of its network cards, learnt from the Station at every contact; the wake packet goes out and the computer is checked every 2 s during “Waking the computer…”, 2 min at most; otherwise “Try again”), the profile and its line, and the gear for its settings. At the bottom, “Your computers”: each one's name and state (long press: Connect, Settings, Remove), those announced “On your network”, “Add a computer”. At the top: the QR code, adding and “App settings”. The figures (latency, graphics card, address) are in the Statistics section. With the iPad keyboard: ↩ connects, ⌘, opens the computer's settings, ⌘N adding, the arrows change computer.
- **Adding**: from the Mac (iCloud), on the network (Bonjour), six-digit code accepted in KyberNow Admin (shown only to the Stations found on the network, valid for five minutes), address, or QR code through the camera (`kybernow://` link, also received through AirDrop or Messages); fingerprint checked on its own when the link or the announcement carries it.
- **Launch**: the system's still image (ink, unlit symbol), then 1.8 s of lighting up in the app; with “Reduce Motion”, a fade.
- **Connecting**: the home screen cut along the 45° seam, the neon tube flickering then lighting up; at the bottom, under the thumb, the three steps (“Preparing the display”, “Connecting”, “First frames”) and “Cancel”; from the first frame, the halves part onto the image alone, with nothing on top. If the picture does not come, the core restarts the Station once and reconnects: “The Station is restarting…” then replaces the steps, which resume afterwards. A failure: “The connection failed”, then the reason. On iPhone, the session is always in landscape.
- **Session**: the image full frame, a discreet tab at the top (no figures), the bar by touch or by swiping down from the top edge (“Statistics”: the live latency and its curve, which open the measurements; profile, sound, trackpad or direct touch, mouse Auto / Desktop mouse / Game mouse, the computer's display to show when it has several without a virtual display, Keys, Keyboard, On-screen controller, Disconnect). Trackpad: one finger moves the computer's pointer, tap = click, two fingers = right click, two fingers sliding = wheel, hold = drag. Direct touch: the finger is the pointer. Keyboard: from the bar or the button under the right thumb; above the keyboard, a row of keys (Esc, Tab, sticky Ctrl, Alt and ⊞, arrows, F1 to F12, Ctrl + Alt + Del); text goes as Unicode; a touch on the picture folds it away, without clicking on the computer (the other gestures keep their effect). Keys menu (Ctrl + Alt + Del, Alt + Tab, ⊞ + D, Print Screen, Alt + F4, ⊞ + L, Task Manager, F11). Bluetooth controllers (L1 + R1 + Start + Select opens the bar and the controller drives it), the iPad's keyboard and mouse or trackpad (iPadOS pointer hidden, the computer's drawn). Statistics in a sheet on iPhone, in a panel on iPad.
- **On-screen controller** (the bar's “On-screen controller” button, remembered for each computer; [docs/link-ui.en.md](../../docs/link-ui.en.md), “With touch”): a fixed Xbox controller over the picture, for both thumbs in landscape. “Full” layout: at the top the LT and RT triggers (above) and LB and RB; on the left the stick, then the D-pad lower and further in; on the right A, B, X, Y, then the right stick lower and further in; at the top centre, either side of the bar's handle, View, Guide, Menu and the small layout button. “Simple”: the left stick, the D-pad, A, B, X, Y and Menu, in the same places. The small button switches between them (accessible label “Simple controller” or “Full controller”) without unplugging the controller; whatever disappears is released. Tapping a stick twice clicks it (L3, R3) for as long as the finger stays; a trigger follows the height of the finger (0.3 at the top, fully in at two thirds), the thumb slides from one A B X Y button to another, the D-pad has eight directions. Touch areas reach beyond the drawing; several fingers at once (both sticks and buttons). Every press lights up (Spectre signal) and sinks slightly; haptic feedback (Core Haptics prepared when shown, short ticks, never a continuous vibration): a click on every button, the D-pad and a clicked stick, a light click at the start of a trigger's travel and a firm one at the end, a tick when a stick leaves the centre, a very light tick at each change of direction (eight sectors), a tick at the edge. The computer sees it as an Xbox 360 controller (045E:028E, whatever the “Controller seen by the computer” setting), in the first free player slot (P1 to P4, otherwise waiting like a fifth controller), plugged in when shown and unplugged when removed; the game's rumble vibrates the iPhone while it is shown. While it is there, the rest of the screen does not act as a mouse (the iPad's trackpad and mouse still do); the handle and the swipe from the top edge still open the bar, and a touch on the picture folds the keyboard away or closes the bar. Capsules narrow between the triggers; the keyboard button under the right thumb gives up its place (the keyboard stays in the bar). On iPhone, the controller takes the whole height (below the bar on the console); on iPad, it sits at the bottom, in landscape and in portrait. It covers neither the notch, nor the Dynamic Island, nor the gesture bar, and shrinks when the screen lacks room.
- **States**: in a capsule at the top of the image, never an alert, and only when something happens: reconnection (neon lit, Disconnect), computer mode changed (“Restore” in the Desktop profile), fallback when the image does not get through, controller connected.
- **External display** (USB-C or AirPlay, iPhone as well as iPad; not tried yet): the image moves to the connected display, at its definition (“like this display” then targets the external one, the virtual display is remade at its size), and the device becomes the console: its whole surface is the trackpad, the bar stays open, measurements are shown on iPad. Connected during a session, the image moves without cutting the session; disconnected, it comes back to the device (“The image is back on the iPad”). Outside a session, the external display shows the symbol and a waiting line.
- **Stage Manager** (not tried yet): a single window; the session lives in its window, the image keeps its format (ink bands), the definition stays the one chosen at the start. Losing the foreground (another window, another app) releases everything on the computer, caps the bitrate and freezes the image (“KyberNow Link keeps running in the background”). No “full screen” button in the bar: iPadOS does not let an app enlarge its window.
- **Background**: everything is released on the computer, the bitrate capped at 8 Mbit/s, the image frozen (except on an external display, which is still being watched); on return, everything comes back.
- **Computer settings** (“Settings for {name}”, the gear on the home screen): the profile (Game, Desktop, Max, Custom) at the top, then the six shared sections, as tabs on iPhone, as a column on iPad (each with a plain summary: “Match this display · 60 Hz”, “Stereo”, “Trackpad”, “Fast”, “Yesterday”, “Up to date”). **Picture**: resolution, refresh rate, virtual display, video format, sharp text (4:4:4), bitrate (the session's, for the current resolution), frame display (Synced or Immediate), keep the last image. **Sound**: Auto, Stereo, 5.1, 7.1 or On the computer, spatial audio in AirPods, late sound alert. **Controls**: touch, pointer speed, mouse, on-screen controller (Layout: Full or Simple; Size: Small, Medium or Large; Opacity, from 20 to 100%; Haptic feedback: Off, Light, Medium or Strong, replaced on iPad by a line saying there is no haptic engine), controller seen by the computer, rumble, bar from the controller, ⌘ sent as Ctrl, Apple ISO keyboard, shared clipboard. **Network**: picture transport, reconnect automatically, Wake on LAN (“Ready” and the MAC address learnt from the Station, or no address yet; waking must also be allowed on the computer, in Admin › System; since iOS may refuse the broadcast, the packet also goes straight to the last known address). **Statistics**: the Mac app's “Last session” card (median in large type, frame ruler from 0 to 20 ms with the 160, 120 and 60 Hz marks and a band from the median to the 95th percentile, “95% of frames under …”, duration and day, resolution, refresh rate, format and bitrate sent), then “This computer”: system, graphics card, address, fingerprint (and, once the Station is reinstalled, the fingerprint presented, to compare before trusting it). **Station**: its version (“Up to date”, “Version … available”, or the installer to run on the computer), “Open Admin” (in the browser), “Restart the Station”, “Update the Station” when a version installs from a device, followed by the app until the Station is back (“The Station is restarting…”, then “The Station has restarted.”); a device in a session asks for “… anyway”; a Station 0.4.9 or older offers neither. Then the account (“This device's own access” when added by code, according to Admin or this device; a new password when Admin refuses the old one) and “Remove this computer”, with confirmation. **App settings** (at the top of the home screen): iCloud, language, sessions on mobile data, what the device can do (display, hardware decoding, sound output and its delay, network), version. Each setting lives in a single place.
- **Mobile data**: “Sessions on mobile data” (app settings), off by default. When off, connecting on mobile data or through a personal hotspot (a path iOS marks “expensive”, `NWPathMonitor`) first opens an alert: Connect, Always allow (turns the setting on), Cancel. Ethernet and ordinary Wi-Fi ask nothing; neither does switching to mobile data during a session.
- **Last session**: at the end of every session of at least 3 s, the app keeps for the computer the median, 95th percentile, duration, date, resolution, refresh rate, format and bitrate (`Host.recordSession`); computers saved by an earlier version still load.
- **KyberNow Share** (contract: [docs/kybernow-share.en.md](../../docs/kybernow-share.en.md); texts: [docs/link-ui.en.md](../../docs/link-ui.en.md), “Sharing”; component shared with the Mac and Apple TV: [apps/macos/README.en.md](../macos/README.en.md), “KyberNow Share”). **Guest**: “Join with a code” (home card, next to “Add a computer”; header button on iPad; home button with no computer) opens the six-digit code screen (number pad, grouped by three), the name screen (the last one used, otherwise the device name) and the **“Picture quality”** choice (under the name: “Auto” suggested, “Same as host”, “4K · 60 Hz”, “1080p · 60 Hz”, “720p · 30 Hz”; five on one line when the width allows, otherwise three then two (iPhone and iPad in portrait), and two per line if even that does not fit; remembered for next time; “Auto” sends the real pixels and refresh rate of the device's screen and what its decoder keeps up with, and the Station decides); at the top, the “Code” / “QR code” choice: “QR code” opens the camera (permission is only asked then) on the host's QR code, which fills in the code and the computer like a link; the code goes to the computers found on the network (“Looking for the share…”, the core's refusals turned into sentences: unknown code, full, too many attempts; the host's format no longer turns anyone away, the Station makes a picture the device can play). A `kybernow://join…` link (Messages, AirDrop) opens the same screen with the code filled in, and nothing is sent before “Join” (not even the Bonjour browse); a share's QR code read by the camera in “Add a computer” leads there too. The guest session is an ordinary session (`SessionController(guest:)`, one at a time), titled “Guest on {name}”, adding nothing to the computer list (no account, no last picture, no statistics); reduced bar: “Spectator” (no right) or “Player” (at least one right), “Picture quality” (menu: the quality received, “1080p · 60 Hz” when the core can say it, otherwise the quality chosen; the five choices, changed during the session without touching the host's picture; the core's notice “Picture lightened to keep up.” or “The computer can't make another picture: you get {quality}.” as a brief capsule), measurements and sound, the on-screen controller only with the controllers (it withdraws when the host takes them back), Keys, Keyboard, mouse and touch only with the keyboard and mouse given by the host (the on-screen keyboard folds away when it takes them back), “Leave”; brief rights-change notices (“You can play with a controller.”, “The controllers were taken back.”, “You have the keyboard and mouse.”, “The keyboard and mouse were taken back.”; no right left: “You are watching again.” in place of the taken-back notice; both rights at once: a single notice, the right given before the right taken back, the controllers before the keyboard and mouse); the end by the share (“{host} stopped sharing.”) on screen, never “Take back control”. **Host**: “Share” in the part of the bar that does not scroll (with “Hide the bar” and disconnect; inactive and dimmed when the Station is too old or sharing is off: the panel says why), opens the share of the primary display and its sheet: the code, big, the QR code, “Copy link” (the web client's address; if copying fails, “Could not copy: select the link by hand.” stays up with the address, selectable), guests (“Léa · Spectator”, “Léa · Player”, “Léa · Keyboard and mouse”, “Léa · Player, keyboard and mouse”, app, picture quality (“1080p · 60 Hz”, read-only) only when it is not the host's, “for under a minute”, “for 3 min”, “for 1 h 12”; two independent rights, each given or taken back without touching the other: “Give” or “Take back controllers”, then “Give” or “Take back keyboard and mouse”; “Remove”), “New code”, “Stop sharing”; the “2 guests” badge sits next to “Share” and stays on the tab when the bar is closed: it reopens the panel.
- **Away from home** (relay; shared component described in [apps/macos/README.en.md](../macos/README.en.md), “Away from home (the relay)”): “Join a share” becomes “Waiting for the host to accept…” with “Cancel” when the host is away; home says “Away from home” for a computer that can only be reached through the relay (home state, cards and settings); the session tab shows “Via the relay”, “1 request” and “2 guests” (the bar: the chip in its fixed part); the “Path” statistics tile; the host panel has its “Requests” section (“Accept”, “Decline”). Before opening the tunnel on a mobile network, a `URLSession` request to the relay's `/v1/health` wakes the cellular radio (`Relay.wakeRadio` in `SessionController.start`, presence and “Join”). Demo: `-demoState away`, `-demoState offline -demoProblem accessoff|asleep`, `-screen join -demoJoinWaiting YES`, `-demoRelay YES`, `-demoRequests 1|2`, `-demoShareRelay unreachable`, `-demoSheetClosed YES`. Verified on the iOS 27.0 simulator (iPhone 16 Pro, iPad Pro 13-inch) on 28/10/2026; not verified: the cellular radio of a real iPhone, a real relay.

## Code

The KyberKit module is the Mac's and the Apple TV's, core included: the session (`SessionEngine`), Admin (`ConsoleClient`, `ConsoleClientCore.swift`), inputs (`InputCore`: kynput chain, pointer held by the app, keyboard, combination, clipboard through `UIPasteboard`), sound (`AudioPlayerCore`, RemoteIO output), latency, VideoToolbox decoding and Metal rendering come from `apps/macos/KyberKit`; the Metal surface, Bluetooth keyboard and mouse (`KeyboardMouse`) and gamepads (`GamepadBridge`) from the Apple TV (`apps/tvos/KyberKitTV`). iOS's own: `KyberKitIOS/StreamView.swift`, the touch session view (gestures, on-screen keyboard as `UIKeyInput`, sticky keys, trackpad hover; `touchAsMouse` false while the on-screen controller is shown), which can show the image in an `ImageHostView` placed on another screen (`showImage(in:)`); `KyberKitIOS/TouchRules.swift`, what a single touch does (fold the keyboard, close the bar, nothing, or click). The on-screen controller is a `GamepadBridge` slot without a GCController (`setScreenPad`, `screenPadButton`, `screenPadStick`, `screenPadTrigger`): the same packets and the same precautions as the others (announced once inputs reach the computer and at each reconnection, everything released on the menu, in the background and at the end of the session, which removes it), rumble through the device's engine.

Display pitfall: on iOS, a Metal layer that presented frames before being in a window stays off screen forever (all its frames discarded, `presentedTime` 0, until the app goes to the background; tried on 22/10/2026 on iPhone). `MetalSurface` therefore creates its own once in the window and attaches the rendering at that point, and remakes it if the display discards everything for two seconds (log `affichage bloqué`, three times at most). `KN_PRESENT_STATS=1` (environment variable at launch through `devicectl`) writes every second the frames rendered, shown and discarded.

The app (`KyberNowIOS/`): `KyberNowApp` (with `AppDelegate`: orientation, external display scene), `AppModel`, `Platform` (screen, sound, preferences, computer images), `Theme` (the Apple TV's tokens at thumb scale), `HomeView` and `HomeParts`, `LaunchView`, `AddHostView`, `AddHostPanes` and `AddHostScanner` (camera), `SettingsView`, `SettingsControls` and `SettingsStats` (“Last session” card, frame ruler), `AppSettingsView`, `SessionController` (the session as seen by the interface, on `SessionEngine`), `SessionView` (transition, connecting screen, bar, key row, Keys menu, capsules, console), `SessionStats`, `ExternalDisplay` (the external display's non-interactive scene, declared by `AppDelegate`), `ScreenPad` (the on-screen controller: layer drawing, tracking of each finger, haptic feedback) and `ScreenPadLogic` (without UIKit: layout, sticks, triggers, D-pad, ticks). The model (`Model.swift`), discovery (`Discovery.swift`) and the wording and rules shared by the Apple apps (`LinkUI.swift`: home, Statistics, Station and its follow-up) come from the Mac app; iCloud sync (`CloudSync.swift`) and computer images (`Backdrops.swift`) from the Apple TV.

## Building

```sh
# libkynput for iOS (once):
../core/scripts/build-apple.sh --target aarch64-apple-ios
../core/scripts/build-apple.sh --target aarch64-apple-ios-sim

# The Rust core, its C bridge, libopus and kynput's header (after every change to apps/core or apps/apple):
../apple/scripts/build-apple-mobile.sh ios

# Xcode project (XcodeGen):
xcodegen generate
open KyberNowIOS.xcodeproj
```

`build-apple-mobile.sh ios` builds `kybernow-core` and `kybernow-apple` as static libraries (iPhone and simulator), adds libkynput, libopus (built with cmake) and VLC's empty functions that kyclient references without ever calling them, and puts it all into `Vendor/KyberNow.xcframework`, with `kybernow.h` (`CKyberNow` module) and `kynput.h` (`CKyber` module, view layout and gamepad constants).

In Debug in the simulator: `-demo YES` loads the demo computers from `LinkUI.swift` (separate preferences, passwords in memory, no network calls, iCloud left aside), with `-demoEmpty YES`, `-demoState online|offline|searching`, `-demoStation current|update|setup|installing|old|restarting|updating`, `-demoProblem password|certificate`, `-stationInSession YES`, `-demoWaking YES` (waking in progress); `-hostIndex 0|1|2` picks the computer; `-skipLaunch YES` skips the launch; `-screen add|settings|app|connecting|restarting|session|bar` opens a screen (the demo session shows a test pattern, without a computer), `-section picture|sound|controls|network|statistics|station` a settings section; `-addMethod 0…4` opens an adding path, `-addStage account` its account step; `-scrollTo screenPad` scrolls the settings down to the on-screen controller. On-screen controller: `-demoPad full|simple` (shown), `-demoPadSize small|medium|large`, `-demoPadOpacity 0.2…1`, `-demoPadHaptics off|light|medium|strong`, `-demoPadPress a,lt,dpad,leftStick` (controls shown pressed); `-demoKeyboard YES` brings up the keyboard.

## Languages

French and English ([docs/i18n.en.md](../../docs/i18n.en.md)): French when iOS is in French, English for any other language. App settings › Language: Auto, Français or English, applied at the next launch. Texts live in the string catalogs (`KyberNowIOS/Localizable.xcstrings`, `InfoPlist.xcstrings`, and `apps/macos/KyberKit/Localizable.xcstrings` for KyberKit). After adding texts: `xcrun xcstringstool sync <catalog> --stringsdata …` from the build's `.stringsdata` (`xcodebuild` does not fill the catalog itself), then `python3 ../macos/scripts/check-strings.py --fix`.

## Signing

Team P6DB682S4K, identifier `fr.leslignesclaires.kybernow`. Capabilities: iCloud (key-value store), Keychain sharing. Distribution: from a Mac, [SIDELOAD.en.md](SIDELOAD.en.md) (`scripts/sideload.sh`, signed with your own Apple account, `KyberSDK-iOS.zip` SDK attached to releases from 0.4.0 on); no App Store profile.

In Debug, manual signing with the “iOS App Development” profile `ios.fr.leslignesclaires.kybernow` (created on 2026-10-21, devices registered in the account), Apple Development certificate. For Xcode to find it, copy the downloaded file to `~/Library/Developer/Xcode/UserData/Provisioning Profiles/<UUID>.mobileprovision`; a new device has to be added to the profile, which is then downloaded again. Installing on a connected device:

```sh
xcodebuild -scheme KyberNowIOS -configuration Debug -derivedDataPath build -destination 'generic/platform=iOS' build
xcrun devicectl device install app --device <UDID> build/Build/Products/Debug-iphoneos/KyberNow.app
```

Release uses automatic signing; it is what `scripts/sideload.sh` builds, also removing the Debug manual signing.

## Tests and limits

- Session checked on an iPhone 15 Pro Max (22/10/2026) and on an iPad. The simulator only shows a test pattern: no hardware decoding.
- On-screen controller and the keyboard that folds away: checked on an iPhone 15 Pro Max (25/10/2026): controller seen by the computer, haptic feedback, keyboard folded away by a touch; not tried yet on iPad. `tests/screen-pad/run.sh` checks, without a simulator, the layout (safe areas, handle, overlaps, sizes), the sticks and their ticks, the triggers, the D-pad, the clicked stick and the touch that folds the keyboard away.
- KyberNow Share: built for the iOS 27.0 simulator (iPhone 18 Pro); “Join” screen with the “Code” / “QR code” choice, guest bars (spectator, controllers, keyboard and mouse), tab with the “3 guests” badge, host sheet with four guests seen in the demo (`-demo YES -screen join|guest|bar`, `-demoRole spectator|player|kbm|player_kbm`, `-demoShare YES`, `-demoRoleThen spectator|player|kbm|player_kbm|end-stopped|end-removed|lag|limit`). **Independent rights** (27/10/2026, iPhone 16 Pro, iOS 27.0 simulator): one guest per role in the sheet; a guest's menu, its two “Give” / “Take back” buttons tried one after the other in the demo (keyboard and mouse given, then controllers taken back: each changes its own right without touching the other); the bar of a guest with the controllers (“On-screen controller”, no mouse or keys) and of a guest with keyboard and mouse (mouse, keys, no controller). **Picture quality** (27/10/2026, iPhone 18 Pro, iOS 27.0 simulator, `-demoRoleThen lag|limit`): “Join” with the five choices (three then two in portrait on the iPhone 16 Pro and the 13-inch iPad Pro, iOS 27.0 simulator, “4K · 60 Hz” included; the line of five was not seen in landscape), the guest bar with “1080p · 60 Hz”, the “The computer can't make another picture…” notice as a capsule, the host sheet (the quality under Léa, with a picture of her own, not under Hugo who follows the host nor Zoé, size unknown); the bar menu (SwiftUI Menu) was not opened. **Not verified**: a real share (the KyberNow Share Station could not be tried yet), changing quality during a share and “Auto” against a real Station, the camera on a share's QR code (the simulator has no camera: only the “No camera available…” message is provided for), the `kybernow://join` link opened from Messages, a player's on-screen controller, iPad.
- External display and Stage Manager: not tried yet. The simulator cannot do it (Xcode 27.1 without Simulator.app; `simctl io … screenConfig` connects a display, but iOS mirrors the screen without giving the scene to the app). If iOS mirrors the screen instead of opening the external scene, set `UIApplicationSupportsMultipleScenes` to `true` in `project.yml`.
- The device's clipboard is only read when the computer asks for it (⌘V or Ctrl+V over there): reading it makes iOS ask for permission to paste, which takes the foreground away from the app. Changes are announced without reading (`hasStrings`).
