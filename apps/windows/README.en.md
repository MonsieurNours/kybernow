# KyberNow Link for Windows

English · [Français](README.md)

KyberNow Link, KyberNow's streaming app for Windows 10 (22H2) and 11: your computer (KyberNow Station: Kyber 0.29 server + KyberNow Admin) in full screen on a PC, with image, sound, keyboard, mouse and gamepads. Target: a laptop or low-powered computer connecting to a more powerful one. Decisions: the “Windows client” row of [PROJECT.en.md](../../PROJECT.en.md); mock-ups: https://claude.ai/artifact/JkUTEMQmxKjcYksaXRHgBf.

The app opens on its home screen (Tauri 2, Svelte interface in WebView2): your computers, adding them, their settings, the passage into the session and the session bar. The session itself is a Win32 window in Direct3D 11; the command line is there for testing. The app also lives in an icon next to the clock (notification area): closing the home screen hides it, and it keeps following your computers from there.

The app rests on the **Rust client core** ([`apps/core`](../core), crate `kybernow-core`), shared by all KyberNow Link apps (the Apple apps reach it through a C bridge: [docs/coeur-apple.en.md](../../docs/coeur-apple.en.md)).

## What the app does

### Outside a session (Svelte interface, `ui/`)

- **Home** (shared screens and texts: [docs/link-ui.en.md](../../docs/link-ui.en.md)): the chosen computer in full frame, its image (the last image of your last session, kept on this PC in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\KyberNow\images` and optional in its settings, “Keep the last image”; otherwise a preview of its display requested from Admin, at most once a minute; arriving with a 45° sweep) or else its **signature** (two glows whose hue comes from its Admin fingerprint), desaturated when offline; then, in this order and nothing else: its state (“Online”, “Offline”, “Searching…”), its name, its system, an error if there is one, the display the session will ask for (“1920 × 1200 display at 60 Hz, sized for this screen”, “3 displays, sized for this device's screens” when the session takes several, or “The computer's own displays, as they are” without a virtual display; same computation as the session: the core for the number of displays (`displays_info` command), `sessionPlan` in `ui/src/lib/api.ts` for `src/plan.rs`), **Connect** (offline: **Wake the computer** when the app knows its network cards, learnt from the Station at each poll, then “Waking the computer…” and the computer polled every 2 s, 2 min at most, until it is back; otherwise **Try again**, which polls it at once), the profile (Game, Desktop, Max, and “Custom” when the settings depart from it) with its sentence, and **Settings**; at the bottom your computers (name and state), those announced on the network (“On your network”, they light up as they appear) and the add card. No graphics card, address or latency: they are in the computer's settings. Admin is polled every 8 s.
- **Adding a computer**: find it **on the network** (Admin's Bonjour announcement `_kybernow._tcp`, fingerprint included; an announcement marked `kyber=0`, Admin with no Kyber server behind it, is not offered), **with Admin's pairing link** (`kybernow://…`, pasted) or **by address** (Admin's fingerprint is then shown, the first four groups to compare with Admin's); then get access **with a six-digit code** to accept in Admin (Link devices: the device gets its own account, nothing to type) or **with an account** and its password, checked with Admin. No camera QR code.
- **Computer settings** (“Settings for Bureau-Salon”; saved on each change): its name (editable), the **profile** (Game, Desktop, Max, Custom: a setting that departs from the profile, format, sharp text, bitrate or transport, makes it “Custom”), then six sections in a column on the left (in a row above the content below 1040 px wide):
  - **Picture**: resolution (match this display, 4K at most, or **custom**: width, height and refresh rate of your choice, up to 7680 × 4320 (4096 wide in H.264), with common values), **displays** (Auto, This display, All displays: see “Several displays” below; inactive, on “This display”, with a custom resolution or without a virtual display, as the core decides), virtual display, video format (Auto, HEVC, H.264, AV1; those the card decodes), sharp text (4:4:4), bitrate (Auto shows the session's real bitrate, same rule as `src/plan.rs`), frame display (synced or immediate), keep the last image;
  - **Sound**: Auto, stereo, 5.1, 7.1 or on the computer;
  - **Controls**: controller seen by the computer (Xbox 360 or as is), mouse (Auto, desktop, game), what the keyboard does in full screen, shared clipboard;
  - **Network**: picture transport (fast or reliable), reconnect automatically, Wake on LAN (“Ready” and the MAC address learnt from the Station, otherwise “No address yet: connect to this computer once.”; no manual entry);
  - **Statistics**: the last session (large median, a ruler from 0 to 20 ms with the 160, 120 and 60 Hz marks and the band from the median to the 95th percentile, “95% of frames under …”, duration and when, resolution, refresh rate, format and bitrate sent) and this computer (system, graphics card, Admin's address, first four groups of the fingerprint);
  - **Station**: KyberNow Station version (“Up to date” or “Version … available”), **Open Admin** (its address in the default browser), **Restart the Station** and **Update the Station** when a version installs from a device (a version that needs the installer says so), account (“This device's own access” for a computer added with a code: the app remembers it, and Admin also says so for those added before 0.5.0), **Remove this computer**. A device in a session: confirmation (“Apple TV du salon is connected”, “Restart anyway”). While waiting, the app polls the Station every 2 s (`poll_now`) until it is back, then says so (“The Station has restarted.”, “KyberNow Station 0.5.1 is installed.”, or “The Station is taking a while to come back…” after 2 min, “The update did not complete…” if it comes back on the old version); a Station older than 0.5.0 (no `station` in `info`): “first update it from Admin”.
- **Passage**: the home screen split along the 45° seam, a neon tube lit during preparation (capsule: preparing the display (“Preparing the displays” with several), connecting, first frames, Cancel; nothing else, no latency or figure; when the picture does not come, the core restarts the Station once as a last resort and the capsule says “The Station is restarting…” before resuming its steps), then, at the first frame shown (not as soon as connected), the session window shows in full screen and the home screen hides, always; with no frame after 12 s, the passage stays on “First frames” with Cancel and says the computer is not sending any image (its log: Admin, System › Logs); left in front, it would show the passage opened onto black. If Windows refuses the foreground to the session (launch from the taskbar, a notification, a link or a remote tool), it briefly attaches to the foreground thread to take it, otherwise it flashes in the taskbar; a session to this PC itself is also refused by address (one of its network adapters' addresses); at the end it comes back, the halves close and the last session is recorded. Several displays: see “Several displays” (the passage is read on the main display, the other displays show the connection background).
- **Session bar**: a transparent window above the session, opened when the pointer **stays 0.25 s against the top edge** of the session window (2 points at most, scaled to the display), in a centred zone as wide as the bar (its real width, which the bar measures and tells the app; an estimate before its first measurement); only if the session is in the foreground, the pointer is on it, the mouse is in desktop mode (not the games mouse) and no mouse button is held (a window on the computer dragged against the top edge). Passing quickly does not open it: a window timer (`TIMER_EDGE`) waits, since a still pointer sends no more movement, and checks everything again when it fires (pointer in the zone, foreground, mouse mode, buttons); it is cancelled if the pointer leaves the zone, the session loses the foreground or switches to the games mouse. A **line** 220 points wide (scaled to the display) by 3, a white capsule at 35% opacity, centred at the top of the window, marks the spot, as on Mac: it is drawn by the session's Direct3D render pass (`render.rs`, `ps_hint`, over the picture, not in the home screen's snapshot), only when the gesture is possible (foreground, desktop mouse, bar not shown); a still picture is redrawn within 100 ms when it changes. The bar is placed at the top edge of the session's client area, centred on it (in full screen, the top of the screen); it closes **0.4 s after the pointer has left it** (it and the strip against the top edge above it), never while one of its menus is open (the keyboard then returns to the session). When requested by “Statistics” (icon menu), it stays until the pointer has been over it. Contents: lit symbol, live **statistics** (latency and its curve, resolution, frames per second, decoder); **profile** (Game, Desktop, Max menu: saved for this computer and applied at once, new bitrate right away or stream restarted if the format, 4:4:4 or the transport change, as on Mac); **Keys menu** (Ctrl + Alt + Del, ⊞ key alone, Alt + Tab, Print Screen); mouse, full screen (not with several displays), **Disconnect**. An open menu makes the bar's window grow downwards while it is shown. Several displays: a single bar, on the display where the gesture happens (that of the window where the pointer stays against the top edge, which becomes the active window), the top line on each display; “Statistics” in the icon menu opens it on the pointer's display; its commands apply to the whole session, from any window; its measurements are those of the main display, with the number of displays first (“3 displays · 3840 × 2160 · 160 fps · …”).
- **Passwords** in the Windows Credential Manager (`KyberNow:<id>`), never in a file.
- **Not onto itself**: connecting to this PC is refused (the virtual display would switch off the displays the app is shown on).
- **Device account not yet known to Kyber**: Admin creates the account of a device accepted by code, but Kyber only reads it when it restarts, which Admin postpones during a session or an encode. The app says so (“restart the server in Admin”) instead of a bare refusal.

### Windows integration

- **Icon next to the clock** (notification area; shared screens and texts: [docs/link-ui.en.md](../../docs/link-ui.en.md), “Icon next to the clock”): the KyberNow Link symbol in a single colour, white or black depending on the taskbar theme (it follows its changes), drawn at the size Windows asks for (16 px at 100%, 24 at 150%, 32 at 200%, `glyph.rs`): at rest the upper half outlined and the lower half filled, in a session both halves filled; tooltip “KyberNow Link”, in a session “KyberNow Link · in session on {name}”. Distinct from the KyberNow Station one (coloured tile and badge): a PC that has both shows two icons. Windows 11 puts a new icon in the hidden area (^ arrow): drag it onto the taskbar to keep it in view. A hidden window on its own thread (`tray.rs`), with no extra library.
  - **Clicks**: right, the menu at once; left, the same menu after Windows' double-click delay (an already open menu would swallow the second click); double click, the window. The menu is dark or light like the taskbar.
  - **Menu** (`menu.rs`, built at each opening): in a session, “In session on {name}”, the measurements (“12.4 ms · 60 fps · 80 Mbit/s”, the latency to the tenth as in the bar), Full screen (checked in full screen; absent with several displays), Mouse (Auto, Desktop mouse, Game mouse; the computer's mode checked, once picked it applies to the session at once and stays for that computer), Statistics (the session bar and its measurements, which stays until the pointer has been over it), Disconnect; then your computers in the home screen's order, each preceded by a coloured dot (green online, red offline, amber while waking and while the Station restarts or updates, grey while searching: brand/v2 state colours, light or dark version to match the menu) and followed by its state, with a submenu: Connect (its profile) or Disconnect, Wake the computer or Try again (“Connect” inactive while the Station restarts or updates), Profile (Game, Desktop, Max; Custom checked and inactive), Settings, Open Admin, Restart the Station, Update the Station (version); at the bottom, Open KyberNow Link (in bold: the double click's one), Add a computer, App settings, “Version … available” or “Check for updates” (the app settings open and check), Quit KyberNow Link (a session in progress ends first). While open, its lines follow the app every second (measurements, computers' state) and a computer's submenu is rebuilt when it opens. The commands are those of the home screen, the settings and the bar; restarting or updating the Station asks for the same confirmation when a device is in a session (a Windows dialog: “Restart anyway”, “Cancel”), refusals included.
  - **Window**: closing the home screen hides it, the app keeps running in the icon (a notification says so the first time); closing the session window means disconnecting. During a session, settings or adding asked for from the icon show the home screen over the session.
  - **Open at login** (app settings, checked at the first launch of the version that brings it, `login.rs`): the `KyberNow Link` value of `HKCU\Software\Microsoft\Windows\CurrentVersion\Run` (`kybernow.exe --background`: no window, in the icon only; the Station's is called `KyberNow`), which follows the app's folder at each launch. Disabling it in Task Manager (Startup tab) or in Windows Settings (`…\Explorer\StartupApproved\Run`) unchecks the box and is never undone by the app; checking it again re-enables it.
  - **Following**: without the home screen, computers are polled every 30 s (every 8 s with the home screen shown), and at once when the menu opens. A wake (every 2 s, 2 min at most) and a Station restart or update (every 2 s until it is back, same rules as the Station section had) are followed by the app itself (`follow.rs`), whether asked from the home screen, the settings, the menu or a notification: the home screen, the Station section and the menu show their state.
- **A single app**: a second launch (shortcut, link, taskbar, notification) hands its request to the app already open, which comes to the front, then quits (`Local\KyberNow.App` mutex, named pipe specific to the user and their Windows session); a hidden window shows itself. `--quit` closes the open app (the installer uses it), `--background` shows nothing.
- **`kybernow://` link** (Admin's pairing link and QR code): registered for the user (HKCU) by the installer and at each launch; it opens the add flow with the computer and its fingerprint already checked.
- **Taskbar**: on a right-click of the icon (and in the Start menu for the pinned app), your computers, one click starts the session (`kybernow.exe --connect <id>`), and “Add a computer”. As many computers as Windows shows (its setting for the number of recent items, 10 by default). The list follows additions, removals and renames.
- **Windows notifications**, only when the app is not in front (a window you can see: the icon's hidden one does not count): session ended (by another device, Admin or an error), with a button that starts the session again (“Take back control”, or “Resume” after an error); new app version ready; a Station version ready (“Station update for Atelier”, once per version and per computer; an “Update” button, which goes through the same confirmation as the menu; no button for a version that needs the installer); the computer online after a wake (“Connect” button), or still nothing after 2 min; end of a Station restart or update started from the menu; “KyberNow Link is still here”, once only. A click on the notification opens the app on what it announces (`kybernow://open?id=…`: the home screen on that computer; `kybernow://station?id=…`: its Station section). Windows hands these links over with a slash before the “?” (`kybernow://connect/?id=…`): the app reads both (`link.rs`). Notification identity registered in HKCU (`AppUserModelId\LesLignesClaires.KyberNow`, display name “KyberNow Link”, icon `kybernow.png`).
- **App settings** (gear in the title bar): version, language (Auto, Français, English), open at login, offered versions (stable, or betas too), checking for and installing an update, its release notes; **This PC** (graphics card, what it decodes, display, sound) and **On battery** (save power: 30 Hz at most during sessions, for every computer). A dot on the gear and a banner in the title bar (“Version … available”) say a version is waiting.

### Installation and updates

- **Installer** `KyberNow-Link-Setup.exe` (`setup/`, “KyberNow Link Setup”; the app in 0.3.1 and earlier looks for `KyberNow-Client-Setup.exe`: that name is therefore still published as a copy, until those versions are gone), in the style of KyberNow Station's (`KyberNow-Station-Setup.exe`): borderless window in Rust, WebView2 and Svelte, side column with the KyberNow Link signed logo, under a large “now” symbol whose seam lights up with the steps (Welcome, Installation, Ready); an “About” page (author, included free software and their licences). One file for **x64 and ARM64**: it carries both versions of the app and installs the processor's one (it runs emulated on an ARM PC and installs the ARM64 version there; an emulated x64 app notes it in its log). For the user only, without administrator rights, into `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\KyberNow`.
  - **Welcome**: what it sees of the PC without changing anything (Windows, processor, graphics card, display, network, WebView2, version already installed, app open), “Desktop shortcut” option. An installed version turns the installation into an update (“Update”).
  - **Installation**: closes the app if it is open (`kybernow.exe --quit`, then forcibly after a few seconds); lays the files down next to it (`KyberNow.new`) then swaps them with the old version in one go (a failed update leaves it intact; if the old folder is locked, copies over it); installs WebView2 if missing (Microsoft's official installer, for the user); `kybernow://` link, notifications and shortcuts set up by the app itself (`kybernow.exe --setup-shell [--desktop]`: `KyberNow Link.lnk`; a `KyberNow.lnk` left by a version from before the rename is removed); “Installed apps” (HKCU, display name “KyberNow Link”, key `…\Uninstall\KyberNow`, the one of the versions already installed).
  - **Ready**: “Open KyberNow Link”, and the session shortcuts.
  - `/S`: silent; `/S /UPDATE`: update started by the app (waits for it to close, relaunches it with `--updated`, and reopens it with a message if the installation fails). The installer never keeps the app's folder as its working directory (Windows would refuse to rename it); if the folder stays locked, files are swapped one by one, with rollback. During installation it holds `Local\KyberNow.Setup`: an app reopened meanwhile waits for the end (2 minutes at most). Without WebView2, the launcher offers to install it before opening its interface. Windows programs are called by their full path (`System32`), not from the installer's folder. The footer is also read behind an Authenticode signature (the PE header's certificate table). ARM64: Windows 11 (Windows 10 on ARM does not emulate the launcher's x64). Logs: `%LOCALAPPDATA%\KyberNow\setup.log` (launcher) and `setup-ui.log` (interface).
  - No Authenticode signature: Windows (SmartScreen) warns at first launch.
- **Uninstalling** (“Installed apps”, `uninstall.exe --uninstall`, the launcher without its contents): closes the app, clears the taskbar list, offers to also erase computers, settings and passwords (passwords through `kybernow.exe --forget-all`, then the data folders: `%APPDATA%\KyberNow`, `%LOCALAPPDATA%\KyberNow` and the WebView2 profile), removes shortcuts (`KyberNow Link.lnk`, and `KyberNow.lnk` if one is left), opening at login (the `KyberNow Link` value of the Run key, the Station's stays), registrations and leftovers of an interrupted update (`KyberNow.new`, `KyberNow.old`), then the folder. The installer's internal options write to `kybernow-setup.log`, not to the app's log.
- **Updates**, like the Station (`server/admin/src/update`): the app checks 15 s after it starts then every 6 hours for the latest version published on GitHub, on its channel; nothing is offered without the version's signed manifest (`kybernow-release.json`, minisign, same key as the Station), which must announce the same version and list the installer. The downloaded installer (`KyberNow-Link-Setup.exe`, or `KyberNow-Client-Setup.exe` when an older release's manifest only lists that one; the same for both architectures) must have exactly the manifest's size and SHA-256 fingerprint. “Install and relaunch” runs it silently (`/S /UPDATE`) and closes the app; it waits for the app to close, replaces its files (not the data) and relaunches it (`--updated`, “KyberNow Link X is installed”). Not during a session.

### Session

- **Session** (core, `apps/core/src/session.rs`, the same as on Mac, Apple TV, iPhone and iPad): preparation by Admin (`POST /api/stream/prepare`: virtual display at this PC's display resolution and refresh rate, virtual sound output with the chosen number of channels, in Auto that of this PC's output, stereo if it has none: the sound then plays here as soon as an output appears), connection to Kyber with the same account, Kyber's certificate required as Admin gives it (`kyber_fingerprint`), display choice (waits at most 6 s for Kyber to announce only the virtual display), heartbeat every 5 s (interval given by Admin) carrying the latency (Admin can ask there for the session to end), the computer's foreground polled every second (Auto mouse), clean end (`POST /api/stream/end`, session latency). Recovery: Kyber's automatic reconnection (“Reconnect automatically” setting, Network section), display requested again from Admin if Kyber restarted, image restarted (at most 3 times) after an image pipeline error, keyframe requested as soon as a reference frame is missing (every 300 ms until it arrives). If the packet that announces the stream's format is lost (fast transport, with losses), the decoder for the format requested for the session opens on its own at the first frame; otherwise every frame would be silently dropped and the screen would stay black. The log records the announced format, the parameters, the first data and the first keyframe. As on Mac, a keyframe is requested only after a loss, never before the first data (the encoder starts with one), and a stream with no image 4 s after it starts is restarted (which also restarts Kyber's video service on the computer), at most three times before ending the session with the reason.
- **Admin**: Basic account, `x-kyber-admin` header, certificate pinned by its fingerprint (`sha256:aa:bb:…`, SHA-256 of the certificate). On the command line, without a given fingerprint (`--fingerprint`), the first one seen is shown and remembered in `%APPDATA%\KyberNow\consoles.txt`; a different certificate afterwards stops the connection.
- **Profiles** Game, Desktop and Max, brought down to what the graphics card decodes, and **bitrate** following pixels per second: same rules and values as the Mac app (tested in `apps/core`). A format, colour or bitrate option makes the profile “Custom”. In H.264, the requested display is brought down to 4096 pixels wide at most (NVENC's limit), and a profile's bitrate follows that reduced display.
- **Image**: FFmpeg (Kyber's, statically linked) decodes on the graphics card (**D3D11VA**, never leaving the card; the app gives FFmpeg the Direct3D device and its video interfaces, which FFmpeg 8.1 does not look up by itself: without them it crashes when creating the decoder; without video interfaces, as in a virtual machine, CPU decoding); at launch, the app really decodes a small frame of each format (H.264, HEVC, HEVC 4:4:4, AV1, in `src/probe/`) to learn what the card can do. If the card does not decode a format, or decodes it into a texture format we cannot show, the app decodes on the **CPU** (slice threads only: frame threads add one frame of delay each), except AV1: no AV1 on the CPU, it is only offered when the card decodes it. Direct3D 11 rendering: a shader converts YUV (NV12, 4:4:4 AYUV or three planes) to RGB according to the stream's colour space and range, at window size keeping proportions; “flip” swap chain, at most one frame waiting; every packet is decoded but only the newest frame is shown (if others are waiting, this one is skipped: delay does not build up behind vertical sync). Vertical sync by default; `--no-vsync` presents each frame as soon as it is decoded (tearing allowed when Windows allows it, useful with a G-SYNC or FreeSync display).
- **Sound**: the core's sound engine (`kybernow_core::audio::AudioEngine`, the same as on Mac, Apple TV, iPhone and iPad: libopus, loss concealment, an adaptive buffer from 4 to 60 ms starting at 8 ms, clock drift caught up smoothly, see [apps/core/README.en.md](../core/README.en.md)); WASAPI in event-driven shared mode pulls its samples from its own thread (`AudioEngine::fill`), with the stream's channels put back in Windows order (5.1, 7.1), and gives it the output latency (its buffer plus what Windows reports); Windows converts to the output's format. The output is reopened when the stream changes its channel count or the output fails. Hovering the bar's latency shows the sound delay to the output and the gaps filled in the last second.
- **Inputs**: through the core's input module (`kybernow_core::input::InputPipeline`, the same as on Mac, Apple TV, iPhone and iPad), one pipeline per image start, created for the displayed screen and stopped with it (FlushKeys, losses to the log); the window captures Win32 and Raw Input events and hands them over. Clock: `Instant` (QueryPerformanceCounter).
- **Keyboard**: Windows key codes as they are (set 1, `0xE000` for extended keys, like Kyber's client), without repeats (the computer repeats by itself); Alt and F10 do not open a local menu. **Windows shortcuts**: in full screen and in the foreground, a low-level keyboard hook sends the computer what Windows would keep for this PC (⊞ key and its combinations, Alt+Tab, Alt+Esc, Ctrl+Esc, Menu key); in a window, they stay on this PC. Ctrl+Alt+Del always stays with Windows: Keys menu of the bar.
- **Mouse**: absolute position brought to the computer's pixels, buttons (including both side buttons), wheel. Games mouse (relative, raw movements through Raw Input, pointer hidden and held in the window): automatic when Admin sees a full-screen game and the computer hides its pointer, or forced with Ctrl+Alt+Shift+M. The core's Auto, Desktop, Game rules (`MouseModeRules`, as on Mac): a pointer shown again for less than 0.6 s (menus, flicker) does not leave the games mouse, checked again 0.7 s later.
- **Pointer**: the computer's pointer (shape and hotspot, received and deduplicated by the core's `PointerLayer`) becomes the Windows pointer over the image, **scaled with the image** (this PC's pixels per computer pixel, between 0.5 and 3: it keeps its size relative to the image); a fully transparent shape keeps the previous one; the shape is only rebuilt when it or the scale changes (position packets arrive up to 120 times a second). **In game mode**, when the computer shows its pointer (a game's menus), this PC's pointer draws it where the computer says it is; hidden otherwise.
- **Display requested again**: when the heartbeat says the virtual display is gone (Admin restarted…), it is requested again and the image restarted, at most once every 30 s (as on Mac). The profile reported by the heartbeat follows changes made from the bar.
- **Gamepads and clipboard**: through kynput's client configuration on Windows (SDL for gamepads, seen by the computer as Xbox 360 pads; text and HTML clipboard both ways). The core's clipboard bridge (`ClipboardBridge`, text only) is not plugged on Windows: kynput already installs its own there, the only possible receiver of its pipeline's clipboard packets.
- **Refused packets**: kynput's send queue holds 64 packets and refuses without notice when full; refused keys and buttons are retried every 3 ms, in order, for up to 200 ms (the core's `PacketQueue`, on its `kybernow-entrees` thread), positions, moves and wheel are sent once. The total of losses goes to the log.
- **Nothing stays pressed**: when the window loses the keyboard, the app releases on the computer the keys and buttons it holds there (`release_all`); the release of a key or button whose press was never sent (an app shortcut) is not sent; at the end of the image and of the session, everything is released.
- **Crashes**: written to the log (panics with the stack, native exceptions with module and offset); the previous launch's log is kept (`kybernow.prev.log`). The graphics card probe runs in a separate process (`kybernow --probe-json`, log `kybernow-probe.log`): if it crashes, the app decodes on the CPU. The unstripped executable gives the faulting line (`llvm-addr2line -e kybernow-<version>-windows-<arch>.exe 0x140000000+offset`): `build.sh` keeps a copy per version and architecture in `apps/windows/dist/symbols/` (ignored by git, on the Mac that built it, about 300 MB each; a new build of the same version replaces it).
- **Measurements**: the session bar gives the end-to-end latency (capture on the computer → presentation here, on Kyber's adjusted clock), the resolution, the decoder and frames per second; the session median and 95th percentile go to the log, to Admin and, for a session of at least 3 s, to the Statistics section. The session window title is “{name} · KyberNow Link”; on the command line (no bar), it also carries the step and the measurements.
- **A single display**: the session is shown full screen on the display of this PC where its window is (“This display”: the home window's display, whose mode is the one asked of the computer).

| Shortcut | Effect |
|---|---|
| Ctrl+Alt+Shift+D | Disconnect (also: closing the window) |
| Ctrl+Alt+Shift+F | Full screen or window (a single display) |
| Ctrl+Alt+Shift+M | Games or desktop mouse |

### Several displays

A session can take all of this PC's displays (contract: [docs/multi-ecrans.en.md](../../docs/multi-ecrans.en.md); texts and behaviour: [docs/link-ui.en.md](../../docs/link-ui.en.md), “Several displays”). The core decides the number of displays with the same rules as on Mac and Linux; the app gives it what decides it and shows what it returns.

- **Setting** “Displays” (Picture section, under Resolution): Auto, This display, All displays (`displays` in `computers.json`, `auto`, `this`, `all`), inactive on “This display” with a custom resolution or without a virtual display (the core's `DisplaysChoice`, through the `displays_info` command of `src/ui.rs`). It does not turn the profile into “Custom”. Changed during a session (home screen opened from the icon), it applies to the next session; the profile picked in the bar or the icon menu applies at once, and the core changes the number of displays if needed.
- **This PC's displays** (`src/displays.rs`): `EnumDisplayMonitors`, `GetMonitorInfo` (frame and main display, in real desktop pixels: the app is per-monitor DPI aware), refresh rate from `EnumDisplaySettings`, scale from `GetDpiForMonitor`; mirrored displays count as one. “This display” is the display the home window is on (`MonitorFromWindow`, `system::home_monitor_key`), as on Mac and Linux: a single-display session takes its mode (`system::screen_mode`) and shows on it; with several displays, Windows' main display becomes the session's main display (the taskbar and full-screen games go there). With “On battery: lighten”, 30 Hz at most for every display. Always given to the core (`Session::start_with_screens`), with the setting, “4K at most”, the custom resolution and the number of displays the Station can create (`virtual_display.max_screens` from `info`, one for an older Station): it makes a one-display or several-display session of them, including when the profile changes during the session. To try without a multi-display Station, `KYBERNOW_MAX_SCREENS=4` acts as if the Station created four (like `KN_MAX_SCREENS` on Mac): the windows open, then, the current Station creating only one, they close.
- **Windows** (`src/window.rs`): one borderless full-screen window per display taken, hidden until the first frame of its stream, all on the session's thread; an invisible (“control”) window receives what applies to the whole session (state, the app's orders, displays). The windows other than the main display's have no taskbar entry. All have the same title; closing one of them means disconnecting. No “Full screen” with several displays (bar, icon menu, Ctrl+Alt+Shift+F: the windows stay in full screen); with “This display” it comes back.
- **Picture**: one image thread per display (`src/video.rs`) with its FFmpeg decoder and swap chain, on **a single Direct3D 11 device** (`src/gpu.rs`). Direct3D protects each call, not a sequence of calls (render target, shaders, a decode in progress: the context's state is shared): a single lock, held while an image is drawn and by FFmpeg around each decoded image (`lock` of `AVD3D11VADeviceContext`), stops one display from undoing another's state. Presenting, which waits for the vertical sync, happens outside the lock: the swap chain has its waitable object (`FRAME_LATENCY_WAITABLE_OBJECT`), waited on before drawing, so a 60 Hz display does not hold back the 160 Hz one. The measurements (latency, frames per second) are those of the main display; the core removes the other displays if they stay silent.
- **Connection**: the steps on the main display (“Preparing the displays”), the connection background, with no text, on the others (`src/fonds.rs`, `ui/fond.html`: a full-screen Tauri window, in the colour of the connection screen, which never takes the keyboard), each display switches to its picture at its first frame. The home screen moves to the main display for the connection and returns to its place at the end. A display the computer could not create, or left without a picture, keeps that background with “The computer could not create this display.”. Station with one display, main display alone accepted, session brought back to one display: the other displays' backgrounds close, with no message. A failed connection is read on the main display, the other windows close.
- **Mouse**: with the desktop mouse, each window sends its positions to its display of the computer (`InputPipeline::mouse_on`); during a drag, the display under the pointer counts (the mouse is captured by the window of the click), so that a window of the computer dragged from one display to another follows. The computer's pointer is drawn in the window of its display (one `PointerLayer` per window, `InputPipeline::set_screens`). Auto mouse: the core's rule (`PointerLayer::summary` over every window's layer: hidden only if it is hidden on every display the pointer has already been on). With the games mouse (Raw Input), the mouse is held in the active window. The keyboard hook stays single: it looks at the window that has the keyboard.
- **This PC's displays changed during a session** (plugged, unplugged, layout, mirroring, resolution): half a second after Windows' last announcement (`WM_DISPLAYCHANGE`), the up-to-date list goes to the core (`Session::set_screens`), which redoes the session if the plan changes; the connection starts again at its first step: the home screen covers the main display with the passage, the other displays take the connection background again, each until its first frame. The same when a profile picked during the session changes the number of displays. The windows of displays the session no longer takes close.
- **Measurements**: the last session keeps the number of displays, the main display's resolution and refresh rate and the total bitrate (`LastSession.screens`); the Statistics section and the bar say so first.

## Languages

The app and its installer come in French and English ([docs/i18n.en.md](../../docs/i18n.en.md)).

- **Detection**: Windows display language (`GetUserPreferredUILanguages`); French if it starts with `fr`, English otherwise.
- **Language setting** (app settings): Auto (default, Windows' language), Français, English; saved in `%APPDATA%\KyberNow\app.json` (`language`) and applied at once to both windows (home and session bar), to Rust-side messages, notifications, the taskbar list and the session window title. The installer has no setting: it follows Windows.
- **Admin**: every request carries `Accept-Language: fr` or `en` (the app's language), so Admin answers in that language.
- **Release notes**: only the part in the app's language is shown (the release text is split at the `<!-- en -->` line: French before, English after; the whole text without it).
- **Catalogues** (same keys, French is the source, a key missing in English falls back to French): interface `ui/src/lib/i18n/{fr,en}.json` (`t()` function), the app's Rust side `src/i18n/{fr,en}.json` (`tr()`, `src/i18n.rs`), core `apps/core/src/i18n/{fr,en}.json` (`apps/core/src/i18n.rs`), installer `setup/src/i18n/` and `setup/ui/src/lib/i18n/`. `npm --prefix apps/windows/ui run check` compares the keys of the interface, the app's Rust side and the core, `npm --prefix apps/windows/setup/ui run check` those of the installer's interface, and a Rust test (`same_keys`) those of `setup/src/i18n/`. Logs and the command line (testing, troubleshooting) stay in French.
- **Imitation** in a browser: the browser's language, or `?lang=en` / `?lang=fr` in the address; `?vide` (no computer), `?installateur` (Station update through the installer), `?sansimage` (the computer sends no picture), `?redemarrage` (last resort: the Station restarts while connecting), `?sansreveil` (offline computer with no known network card), `?echecmaj` (the Station update does not complete), `/bar.html` for the bar. One imitated computer per state: online with a last session and a Station update (restart with confirmation, then follow-up), offline and wakeable, up to date with a 0.4.x session, Station too old.

## Usage

`kybernow.exe` without arguments opens the app; `--background` starts it without a window (opening at login), `--quit` closes the open one. On the command line, a direct session (testing, troubleshooting):

```
kybernow <address>[:port] [--user <account>] [--profile jeu|bureau|max] [options]
```

- `<address>:port`: the computer and the Admin port (Link devices, in Admin); 9443 by default.
- Password: `--password`, the `KYBERNOW_PASSWORD` variable, otherwise asked.
- `--codec h264|hevc|av1`, `--444` / `--420`, `--mbps <bitrate>`, `--size 1920x1080@60` (otherwise this PC's main display), `--window` (a single display: a window's display is the size of its window), `--screens auto|this|all` (this PC's displays taken by the session: `this`, a single one, by default; `KYBERNOW_MAX_SCREENS=4` acts as if the Station created four), `--no-vsync`, `--no-hw` (CPU decoding), `--no-audio` (sound left on the computer), `--physical` (the computer's displays as they are), `--no-station-restart` (no Station restart as a last resort when the picture does not come).
- `--probe`: shows what the graphics card decodes, the display, this PC's displays (frame, refresh rate, scale, main), the sound output and the system, then quits. Needs no computer.

## Building

Everything builds from the Mac:

```bash
apps/windows/scripts/build.sh                # x64 (x86_64-pc-windows-gnu, Homebrew MinGW, like the server)
apps/windows/scripts/build.sh --arch arm64   # ARM64, Snapdragon PCs (aarch64-pc-windows-gnullvm, llvm-mingw)
```

Then the installer, once both folders are built: `apps/windows/scripts/build-installer.sh` writes `apps/windows/dist/KyberNow-Link-Setup.exe` (`[launcher][x64.tar.zst][arm64.tar.zst][64-byte footer “KNCLNT01”]`, see `setup/src/payload.rs`). The `setup/` project has its own `Cargo.lock`, with the server installer's versions (`wry`, `tao`, `zstd`), and is built for x64 only. Its interface (`setup/ui`) can be viewed in a browser with a simulation: `npm --prefix apps/windows/setup/ui run dev`, with `?maj`, `?ouverte`, `?arm`, `?echec` in the address. `scripts/release.sh` builds everything and attaches the installer to each version.

The folder to copy to the PC is `apps/windows/dist/x64/` (or `arm64/`), also as an archive `dist/KyberNow-<version>-windows-<arch>.zip`: `kybernow.exe`, `kynput.dll` and `SDL2.dll` (gamepads), `WebView2Loader.dll` (interface), `kybernow.png` (notification icon), plus the toolchain runtime DLLs they ask for (`libunwind.dll` on ARM64). The rest comes with Windows 10 and 11 (Universal C runtime, Direct3D 11, `d3dcompiler_47.dll`). Each version's unstripped executable stays in `dist/symbols/kybernow-<version>-windows-<arch>.exe` (crashes, see above).

- **x64 prerequisites**: `third_party/` in place, and `server/kyber-patches/build.sh` run once (it produces `third_party/rootfs-win64`, with FFmpeg and libopus, and `third_party/rootfs-win64-kynput`, with `kynput.dll`).
- **ARM64 prerequisites**: llvm-mingw in `third_party/toolchains`, the Rust target `aarch64-pc-windows-gnullvm`, then `apps/windows/scripts/build-arm64-deps.sh` once: libopus, an FFmpeg reduced to decoding (H.264, HEVC, AV1, D3D11VA), SDL2 (kynput's verified sources) and `kynput.dll`, in `third_party/rootfs-winarm64`. Tool versions: [docs/build-notes.en.md](../../docs/build-notes.en.md).
- **Kyber patches**: the same as for the Apple apps (`apps/macos/kyber-patches`), applied by the script if they are not.
- **VLC**: kyclient always links it on Windows, but the app decodes by itself: the script replaces each function of `libvlc.dll` with an empty one (as on the Apple TV) and refuses an executable that would still depend on `libvlc.dll`.
- `--debug`: without optimisation.
- **Core and app tests** (on the Mac; for the app, what does not depend on Windows: the icon's menu, follow-ups, the icon's drawing, texts, links, catalogues; `KN_GLYPH_DIR=<folder>` also writes the icon and the dots there as RGBA, to look at them):

```bash
cd apps && PKG_CONFIG_PATH=$PWD/../third_party/kyber-desktop/rootfs-arm64-macos/lib/pkgconfig LIBCLANG_PATH=/Library/Developer/CommandLineTools/usr/lib cargo test -p kybernow-core -p kybernow-windows
```

## Testing

The interface can be worked on in a browser on the Mac, with an imitation of the app: `npm --prefix apps/windows/ui run dev` (or the `windows-ui` configuration in `.claude/launch.json`).

Copy `dist/x64` to the PC (or `dist/arm64` to a Snapdragon PC, or into Windows 11 ARM in a virtual machine on the Mac: it tells whether the app starts and connects, not the latency nor graphics-card decoding), then start `kybernow.exe` (the app), or in a terminal:

```
kybernow.exe --probe
kybernow.exe 192.168.1.100:9443 --user <account> --profile bureau
```

The interface needs Edge's WebView2 runtime, present on Windows 11 and on most Windows 10 machines (the installer installs it when needed).

On first contact, Admin's fingerprint is shown: compare it with Admin's (Link devices). The log is in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\KyberNow\kybernow.log`.

**Several displays**: the interface is imitated with `?ecrans=3` (this PC has three displays: the home screen's line, the steps and the bar in the plural), `?reprise` (during a session, the connection starts again) and `/fond.html?sanscreation` (the background of a display the computer could not create). On a PC with several displays, without a multi-display Station: `set KYBERNOW_MAX_SCREENS=4` then `kybernow.exe` (or `kybernow.exe <address> --screens all`) opens the windows, which close when the Station answers that it creates only one; the log says this PC's displays, those taken by the core, the windows opened and closed (`fenêtre de session`, `fond de la connexion`, `écrans de ce PC`). With a Station that can create several displays: Desktop profile, Displays setting on Auto → one display of the computer per display of this PC, laid out the same way.
