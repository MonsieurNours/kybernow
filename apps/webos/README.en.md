# KyberNow Link for LG TVs (webOS)

English · [Français](README.md)

KyberNow Link on LG TVs: a **native** app (an ARM binary, not a web page), on the same Rust core as the other Link apps (`apps/core`), that brings up the computer (KyberNow Station) full screen, with the Magic Remote, keyboard and mouse, or gamepad. v2 identity ([guidelines](../../brand/v2/README.en.md)); mockups: [Claude Design](https://claude.ai/artifact/2TNZZrYYZpHvdQqLXzSLGv) (home, session menu with the Magic Remote, Picture, Network and Sound settings, controllers and rumble); screens and texts shared by every Link app: [docs/link-ui.en.md](../../docs/link-ui.en.md). Installing on the TV: [INSTALLATION.en.md](INSTALLATION.en.md) (developer mode).

**Status (23/10/2026)**: installed on the G2 (webOS 26) in developer mode; end-to-end session at 4K and 1440p: picture, stereo and 5.1 Dolby sound to a receiver, controller (connected before or after launch), Magic Remote. The binary builds for webOS (armv7, glibc 2.12), so does the `.ipk` package, and the interface is checked through its screenshots (on the computer, and by the ARM binary under emulation). The Statistics and Station sections of a computer's settings are only checked through their screenshots, not yet on the TV. Test TV: LG OLED G2 83 inches (2022). The decision is in [PROJECT.en.md](../../PROJECT.en.md) ("LG TVs (webOS)"). KyberNow Share (joining and sharing): built for the TV (`build.sh --check`) and checked through its screenshots and tests; not yet tried on a TV nor with a real Station 0.8.0 (the host's picture on the guest, "Picture quality" with a real Station: "Auto" measured on the panel, "4K · 60 Hz" (NDL's decoder at 2160p60), NDL's decoder following a size change mid-stream, the sound, a controller that becomes Windows' next controller, the role notices, the panel with real guests, the keypad with the remote, the home row sliding under the Magic Remote with many computers).

## TVs

- **webOS 5 or later** (LG TVs from 2020 on): the NDL DirectMedia decoder in API 2, the one the app uses. Main target: webOS 22 and later (OLED C2, G2 and after), including the webOS Re:New updates (webOS 24 and 25 on 2022 models).
- **Installation**: in developer mode (free LG account, no root); no Homebrew Channel repository. The LG Content Store is not targeted: it does not publish native apps from non-partners.
- **Test hardware**: LG OLED G2 83 inches; DualSense, Xbox, Shield and Switch Pro controllers.

## Picture, frame rate and game mode

- **Session mode**: the panel's (`SDL_webOSGetPanelResolution`, `SDL_webOSGetRefreshRate`), that is 3840 × 2160 at 120 Hz on a G2; or a resolution or frame rate chosen in the settings. The computer's virtual display is created at this mode.
- **30, 60 or 120 frames per second**: the panel does not change its refresh rate for an app (webOS offers no way to); at 30 or 60 fps each frame is shown 4 or 2 times, evenly. 144 Hz: the 2025 panels that have it (not the G2).
- **Codec**: HEVC by default (4K at 120 fps on the decoder of 2022 OLEDs); AV1 only up to 60 fps (beyond, LG TVs show it at 60), and only when the webOS version can be read (5 and later); H.264 as an option, not advised above 60 fps.
- **Bitrate**: the profile's bitrate (scaled to pixels per second, like the other apps), capped at 80 Mbit/s on Ethernet (LG's port stops at 100 Mbit/s, about 93 usable) and at 95 on Wi-Fi (the decoder limit on 2022 and later chips). The link is read from `/sys/class/net`.
- **TV game mode**: no API lets an app switch the TV to Game Optimizer; the menu reminds you (Magic Remote Settings button › Picture › Mode › Game Optimizer), and the reminder can be turned off.
- **VRR**: LG keeps it for HDMI inputs, for every internal app, native or web. The computer sends its frames at the panel's pace.
- **HDR**: no HDR. The decoder can show it (`NDL_DirectVideoSetHDRInfo`, HEVC Main10), but Kyber 0.29 captures in 8 bits, as on the other apps; the app does not call that function.

## Decoding (NDL DirectMedia)

`src/device/ndl.rs`. What matters, checked on the G2:

- `libNDL_directmedia.so.1` opened with `dlopen`, never linked (a direct dependency keeps the app from starting where it is missing).
- `NDL_DirectMediaInit` at the first load, not at app launch.
- Every load carries an **audio plane primed with 5 ms empty frames**: stereo Opus (`0xec 0xff 0xfe`), or 6-channel PCM with the 5.1 PCM setting. Without it, NDL does not pace frames on their timestamps, and the picture stutters above 1080p on a 120 Hz panel. The real sound only goes there in 5.1 PCM (see Sound).
- **The load is only confirmed (`LOADCOMPLETED`) once that plane is fed**: empty frames go out in bursts until the confirmation (in the background past 500 ms), and no frame is given before it (2 s at most). A single frame is not enough: the G2 on webOS 26 then stays on a black picture, with no error. Fed this way, the load is confirmed there in 141 ms.
- Timestamps in milliseconds since the load, never going backwards.
- **Never flush** (`NDL_DirectVideoFlushRenderBuffer`) during a session: it cuts the audio plane for the rest of the session. After a loss, no frame is given before the next keyframe, requested again by the core.
- The parameter set (SPS/PPS, VPS, AV1 sequence header) goes before the keyframe that (re)starts the decoder, and again before the keyframe that follows a new set; another codec or size reloads NDL.
- No NDL function is thread-safe: a single lock.
- NDL does not say when a frame is shown: the display time (latency) is estimated from the frames waiting (`NDL_DirectVideoGetRenderBufferLength`) and the panel's refresh rate.

## Sound

Opus decoded by the core's engine (`apps/core/src/audio`: adaptive buffer, loss concealment), pulled by SDL's callback (the TV's PulseAudio, 48 kHz, 512 frames per callback) in **stereo**: a game in 5.1 or 7.1 is mixed down by the engine. The app asks the computer for stereo (Auto and Stereo). Sound can stay on the computer.

**5.1 Dolby** (Sound › 5.1 Dolby setting): the app asks the computer for 6 channels, decodes the Opus itself, encodes it to **E-AC-3** (Dolby Digital Plus, 640 kbit/s, 1536-sample frames i.e. 32 ms; libavcodec reduced to its ac3 and eac3 encoders, `src/eac3_shim.c`, `src/device/eac3.rs`) and hands it to the TV's decoder, which passes it untouched to the receiver over eARC (Digital Sound Output set to Pass-Through). Checked on the G2 with a Denon AVC-X3700H on 2026-10-23. It is the only road to a receiver: the TV's own apps do the same, and the receiver does not take multichannel PCM (see below).

- **Two chains**: the picture stays on NDL's game decoder (`ndl.rs`, `DIRECTMEDIA-ES-PLAYER` transport, low latency, silent Opus plane pacing it, as in stereo). Sound has its own, **audio-only** chain (`src/device/esplayer.rs`): LG's media player through its C façade `NDL_ES_*` (`libNDL_esmedia.so.1`; signatures read from the machine code, same set of functions from webOS 22 to 26), loaded as `BUFFERSTREAM` (the movie players' chain) with the single codec `AC3 PLUS` and `ac3PlusInfo`, no added buffering, frames stamped in nanoseconds on arrival. Primed with one E-AC-3 silence frame every 32 ms until confirmed (2.5 s at most), loaded after the picture decoder (it takes its decoders first), unloaded before it and at the end of the session. Without a confirmation, or on an error during the session that is not an unload (see below): stereo through SDL for the rest of the session.
- **Why not a single chain**: the game decoder only decodes PCM, MP3 and Opus, and its audio sink crashes on a first Dolby buffer (crash report from the G2, `lgao_push_first_event`). A two-stream `BUFFERSTREAM` chain passes Dolby, but its sinks sync on the GStreamer clock (the game decoder exempts them: `game-mode`, `disable-gstsync`) and the picture locks onto the sound: picture and inputs lag (tried on the G2). With two chains the picture keeps its latency and the sound alone carries its delay (32 ms frame, the TV's chain, the receiver's decoding): a fixed offset, no drift (every frame is stamped on arrival).
- **Unloaded by the TV**: when sound stops (a sound mode change on the computer, for instance), the TV's resource policy unloads both chains by itself (`UNLOADCOMPLETED` with no request). The picture decoder takes note (keyframe requested again, reload), and a refused load reinitialises NDL (`Quit` then `Init`) before trying again: without that, NDL stays in an invalid state and every reconnection fails. The Dolby chain too: when unloaded (its event, or a `NDL_ES_Feed` error after it worked), it reloads in the background, after the picture decoder, primed as on the first load (a refused load takes a new player); frames in between are skipped, and sound only falls back to stereo through SDL if that reload fails.
- **Hook**: the binary exports `StarfishMediaAPIs::Load` and `Feed` (`src/starfish_hook.c`, `src/device/starfish.rs`); LG's libraries, loaded later, resolve them in the app, which logs the load documents and the player's events (`SMP CB`), then hands them untouched to the real player. It is there for diagnosis: the game decoder's document and its events are in `kybernow.log` (`lecteur :` lines).

**5.1 PCM** (Sound › 5.1 PCM setting): 6 channels decoded by the app and given as S16LE PCM to NDL's "6-channel" plane, the TV's multichannel PCM to HDMI. No encoding delay, but the TV only outputs it when the receiver announces multichannel PCM over CEC (short audio descriptors): the Denon AVC-X3700H announces LPCM 2 channels only (AC-3 and E-AC-3 in 5.1 and 7.1), and many receivers do the same. The "TV 5.1 output" line in the settings reads `NDL_DirectAudioSupportMultiChannel`, which fails (-1) on the G2 under webOS 26: "unknown".

- Letting the TV decode the 5.1 Opus itself (6-channel Opus plane) comes out in stereo: its output goes through the TV's mixer.
- **No raw stream to the receiver**: the computer sends Opus, which no receiver decodes.
- **No 7.1**: E-AC-3 5.1 at most through this road (libavcodec's `eac3` encoder stops at 5.1); the Sound screen says so.
- **TV settings**: Sound › Digital Sound Output set to **Pass-Through**; on PCM or Auto everything comes out in stereo, LG's apps included. That setting can revert to PCM after a reboot or a settings change: check it when the receiver falls back to stereo.

## Wake on LAN

Admin gives the computer's network cards (MAC address, IPv4 address, broadcast address) with every successful poll; the app keeps them for each computer in `kybernow.json` (the old ones stay if Admin no longer gives any, with a 0.4.9 Station for instance). Offline, "Wake the computer" sends the wake packet through the core (`kybernow_core::wake`, broadcast and direct), shows "Waking the computer…" and polls the computer every 2 s, for 2 min at most, until it answers. Nothing is typed on the TV: the cards are learnt on the first connection. Waking must also be allowed on the computer (Admin › System).

## Rumble

The game's rumble reaches the core (`InputPlatform::rumble`) and goes to the player's controller through `SDL_GameControllerRumble` (5 s at most without a duration, (0, 0) stops). On LG TVs, Bluetooth controllers rumble when webOS exposes their motors (the DualSense over Bluetooth does not always rumble there); the test screen says "2 motors" or "no rumble" for each controller, and the test rumble checks it. The game only drives the two motors (the core passes no trigger rumble); the test screen also tries the triggers (`SDL_GameControllerRumbleTriggers`) when the controller has them. No DualSense adaptive triggers.

## Architecture

```
apps/webos/
├── src/
│   ├── main.rs          launch (--preview: screenshots on the computer)
│   ├── preview.rs       screenshots of the screens with demo data
│   ├── app.rs           interface state, spatial navigation, actions (no SDL nor core)
│   ├── model.rs         computers, settings, the TV's identity (kybernow.json in Store::dir)
│   ├── i18n.rs          French and English
│   ├── ui/              drawing (tiny-skia, fontdue): home, adding, settings (Statistics,
│   │                    Station and its QR code), controllers, session; join.rs (join a
│   │                    share), share.rs (host panel), quality.rs (a guest's picture quality)
│   ├── device/          the TV: SDL (window, events), NDL (picture), sound, Dolby chain
│   │                    (esplayer, eac3), player hook (starfish), session (the core's Platform),
│   │                    controllers, background jobs (Admin, Station, wake, mDNS, code, updates), log
│   └── *.c              glibc shims, E-AC-3 encoder (libavcodec), hook on LG's player
├── kyber-patches/       kynput patch for webOS (client only)
├── sdl-patches/         patch to webosbrew's SDL2 (controllers switched on after launch)
├── assets/              brand fonts (static instances), logo
├── webos/               appinfo.json, icons, splash screen
└── scripts/             build.sh, package.sh and ipk.py (package), preview.sh (screenshots),
                         fonts.py and images.py (fonts and images taken from the brand)
```

- **Core**: `kybernow-core` as is; `src/device/session.rs` is the equivalent of Android's JNI bridge (the core's `Platform`: picture, sound, input chain, states, stats, pointer, rumble).
- **SDL2**: webosbrew's webOS version (`release-2.30.12-webos.6`), built by `build.sh` with the patches in `apps/webos/sdl-patches` (rescan of `/dev/input` every 3 s on top of the netlink channel, which stays silent in the app's sandbox: without it, a controller switched on after launch is never seen) and shipped in the package (`lib/`, found through the `$ORIGIN/../lib` RPATH, as `DT_RPATH` to come before the TV's older SDL). Settings: Back and long Back to the app (`SDL_WEBOS_ACCESS_POLICY_KEYS_BACK`, `_EXIT`), controllers left as controllers (`SDL_WEBOS_CLOUDGAME_ACTIVE`). SDL's functions are declared by hand (`src/device/sdl.rs`).
- **Interface**: drawn in memory (tiny-skia for shapes and images, fontdue for text) at 1920 × 1080, webOS's graphics plane, then sent to the screen through an SDL texture. During the session, the window is cleared to transparent; the computer's pointer is a small separate texture, recomposited without redrawing the interface.
- **Fonts**: Archivo and Martian Mono from `brand/v2/fonts`, as static instances cut down to Latin (`scripts/fonts.py`), since fontdue does not read variable axes.
- **VLC**: kyvlcplayer references libvlc, never called: `build.rs` compiles an empty function for each `libvlc_*` function of vlc-rs, as on Android and iOS.
- **glibc**: the TV has glibc 2.12; `src/glibc_compat.c` provides `getauxval`, `gettid` and `sendmmsg`, which Rust's standard library asks for.
- **kynput**: on Linux, kynput compiles the server's input injection (libinput, libevdev, X11), missing on the TV; patch `kyber-patches/0001` (cfg `kybernow_webos`) makes it behave as on Android (client only).

## Building

```sh
# Once: Rust target, cargo-c, cmake, Python 3 (and pip install fonttools pillow cairosvg for fonts and images).
rustup target add armv7-unknown-linux-gnueabi
cargo install cargo-c --version 0.10.15 --locked

# The binary and the package (on Linux x86_64 and on an Apple Silicon Mac, the webOS toolchain is downloaded when missing):
apps/webos/scripts/build.sh                  # → apps/webos/dist/fr.leslignesclaires.kybernow_<version>_arm.ipk
apps/webos/scripts/build.sh --check          # cargo check only
apps/webos/scripts/build.sh --no-package     # binary only (--debug: no optimisation)

# The screens on the computer, without a TV:
apps/webos/scripts/preview.sh                # → apps/target/webos-preview/*.png
```

`scripts/build.sh` builds webOS's SDL2 with the patches of `apps/webos/sdl-patches` into `third_party/sdl-webos-dist` (rebuilt when a patch changes), applies the patches of `apps/macos/kyber-patches` then those of `apps/webos/kyber-patches`, builds into `third_party/kyber-desktop/rootfs-armv7-unknown-linux-gnueabi` libkynput (without SDL, `cargo cinstall`), libopus 1.6.1 (position-independent: the binary is PIE), FFmpeg 7.1.1's libavcodec and libavutil reduced to the ac3 and eac3 encoders (static, LGPL 2.1, licence in the package), writes `libvlc.pc`, then builds `kybernow-webos` and packages it (`scripts/package.sh`: binary without debug info, SDL2, images, licences). Kyber 0.29's sources go into `third_party/kyber-desktop` (see [docs/build-notes.en.md](../../docs/build-notes.en.md)).

- **Toolchain**: webosbrew's, downloaded once by `build.sh` (then `./relocate-sdk.sh`) when `WEBOS_TOOLCHAIN` is not set. On Linux x86_64: `openlgtv/buildroot-nc4` (gcc 16, glibc 2.12, armv7 softfp), in `third_party/webos-toolchain`. On an Apple Silicon Mac (tried on 2026-10-23): `arm-webos-linux-gnueabi_sdk-buildroot_darwin-arm64.tar.bz2` from [webosbrew/native-toolchain](https://github.com/webosbrew/native-toolchain) (gcc 12, glibc 2.12), in `third_party/webos-toolchain-mac`. Elsewhere: `WEBOS_TOOLCHAIN` is required. `package.sh` run on its own picks the same toolchain for the machine (without downloading it). `build.sh` adds `/opt/homebrew/opt/rustup/bin` to `PATH` when it exists (rustup from Homebrew). `scripts/cc.sh` gives the compiler its sysroot and links libstdc++ statically.
- **Rust options**: `-C target-feature=+neon,+vfp3,-soft-float -C target-cpu=cortex-a73` (otherwise the "gnueabi" target emulates floating point: ten times slower), `--cfg kybernow_webos`, `--cfg getrandom_backend="linux_raw"`.
- **Package**: `scripts/ipk.py` writes the `.ipk` as `ares-package` does (ar archive: `debian-binary`, `control.tar.gz`, `data.tar.gz` with `usr/palm/applications/<id>/` and `usr/palm/packages/<id>/packageinfo.json`), without LG's tools.
- **Emulation**: `qemu-arm` does not run a binary linked against the TV's glibc 2.12 (its loader crashes, even for a minimal C program); the preview is checked with a static binary (`--no-default-features`, `+crt-static`, the glibc shim as `-C link-arg`, `qemu-arm -cpu max`).

## Updates

`KyberNow-Link-webOS.ipk` is attached to every release (`scripts/release.sh`, covered by the release's signed manifest). At launch then every 6 hours the app (`src/device/jobs.rs`, `kybernow_core::update`, as on Android) looks for the latest release on its channel that publishes it, checks the signed manifest, and announces it: "Version X available" on the home screen, and in App settings › This TV (version, versions offered: stable or betas too). In developer mode an app cannot install itself: the package is installed from the computer ([INSTALLATION.en.md](INSTALLATION.en.md), "Updating").

## Log

`/media/developer/temp/kybernow/kybernow.log` on the TV (the previous one as `.1`), next to `kybernow.json` (computers and settings). In developer mode, `HOME` is `/media/developer`, closed to the app's user, and its `/tmp` is emptied on reboot and invisible over ssh: `temp` is open to all, survives reboots and reinstalls, and can be read over ssh. The app takes the first folder it can write to among `$HOME/conf`, this one and `/tmp/kybernow` (`Store::dir`), and standard error: `image :` (NDL), `son :`, `manettes :`, `session :`, `lecteur :` (hook on LG's player) lines, with the core's messages (logs stay in French). See [INSTALLATION.en.md](INSTALLATION.en.md) to read it.
