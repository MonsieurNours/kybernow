A relative enjoys your gaming PC while you are away from it, a colleague uses the workstation. They play or work from their KyberNow Link app or their browser, at your place or from theirs, without seeing your desktop or your windows.

## How it works

The Station switches your own session to **console mode**: a full-screen launcher that only shows the apps you have allowed. There is no second account to create on the computer. When the loan ends, everything goes back to how it was and you find your session open.

Lending is part of KyberNow Share. It works on a Windows, Mac or Linux Station.

## Turn lending on

1. In Admin, open the Lending page and choose “Turn lending on” (or switch on “Lend the computer”).
2. In “Allowed apps”, keep Steam (in Big Picture) and add what your guest will be able to open with “Add an app”: another game launcher (Epic, GOG, Playnite…), a game, a work program. The apps and their icons come from the computer's list of apps (the Start menu, on Windows). Everything else is blocked.
3. In the “Settings” tab, give “Your first name”: your friends read, for example, “Hugo is lending you their computer.”

While lending is on, your session must stay open: the Station prevents sleep and automatic locking. A locked session blocks lending (“The computer is locked.”).

## Three ways to lend

| Way | How |
|---|---|
| A code for one session | “Lend the computer…” in Admin, in the Station icon's menu or in the session bar of your Link apps. Choose the “Duration” (“1 hour”, “2 hours”, “4 hours” or “Until I take it back”) and the “Steam account”, then “Create a code”. Give out the six-digit code, the link or the QR code. The code works once and is valid for 24 hours. |
| Friend accounts | In Admin, Lending page, “Friends” tab: “Add a friend”, with a first name, a username, a password and a suggested Steam account. Admin gives you an “Invitation link” and its QR code. Your friend opens it, and “Hugo's computer” appears on their app's home screen. From then on they borrow with their username and password, no code needed. |
| Time slots | On a friend's account, “Add a time slot”: days and hours of the week, for example Wednesdays from 2 pm to 6 pm. During their time slot, they borrow without asking you, and take priority over you. |

## The Steam account

If Steam is one of the allowed apps, you choose for each loan:

- “Their Steam account”: Steam signs out of yours and your friend signs in with theirs. They play their games, and yours if they are in your Steam family. The accounts saved in your Steam are set aside.
- “Your Steam account”: they play with your games and your saves.

At the end, Steam goes back to your account.

## On your friend's side

1. With a code: in the KyberNow Link app, “Join with a code”, or the link in a browser.
2. With a friend account: the “Hugo's computer” card on the home screen, then “Borrow”. On the same network, “Add a computer or a console” › “With a friend account” adds it too, with the username and password.
3. If the computer is in use, the button becomes “Ask Hugo” and the app waits for your answer (“Waiting for Hugo…”).

## When your friend arrives

- **The computer is free** (nobody in front of it for 5 minutes, no session, no share): the loan starts.
- **The computer is in use**: you receive “Léa wants to borrow the computer.” in Admin, on the Station icon and in your session bar, with “Accept” and “Decline”. Accepting closes your session.
- **During their time slot**, your friend takes priority: you are warned two minutes beforehand (“Léa's time slot: the computer is lent to them in 2 min.”).

A code created from your session counts as your agreement: your session will close when your friend arrives. One loan at a time.

## During the loan

Your friend gets an ordinary session on their screen, with the keyboard, the mouse and their controllers, but no clipboard. They also get HDR, the microphone and the camera, just as you do. Their bar shows “Lent by Hugo”, the time left (“1 h 20 left”) and “Give back the computer”.

Their launcher, full screen like a console, shows a tile for each allowed app, their “Last played” first, the time left and “Give back the computer”. It can be driven with a controller (“A Open”, “B Back”), a keyboard or a mouse.

On your computer's screen, a cover shows “Computer lent to Léa” and “To take back the computer, press Enter.” During the loan, the Station blocks anything that would let someone leave the launcher: on Windows, File Explorer, the Windows key, Alt+Tab, Task Manager, command prompts, the “Open” and “Save As” dialogs and apps not on the list; on Mac and Linux, terminals, system settings, file managers and apps not on the list.

If their connection drops, your friend can come back within 15 minutes (“Come back”). “Disconnect” closes their session without giving the computer back. During a loan, the screen can't be shared or recorded.

## Take back the computer

1. Choose “Take back the computer” in Admin, on the Station icon or in your Link apps (this computer's home screen says “Lent to Léa”), or press Enter in front of the computer.
2. Your friend has two minutes to save: they read “Hugo is taking back the computer in 2 min: save your game.” “Take back now” ends it without waiting.
3. Their apps close, Steam goes back to your account, your windows come back and the virtual display disappears. You find your session open.

The same two-minute notice comes before the end of the chosen duration or of the time slot.

> Good to know: your files are protected as well as possible, but they aren't out of reach. A game or an allowed app can read your disk. Lend the computer to people you trust.

## If something goes wrong

- **“The computer is locked.”** Your session is locked: unlock it. While lending is on, the Station keeps it open.
- **“Lending is off on this computer.”** Turn lending on in Admin, Lending page.
- **“This code is no longer valid.”** The code has already been used, or its 24 hours are up. Create another one.
- **“Username or password refused.”** Check the friend account in Admin, “Friends” tab; you can change the password there.
- **“Hugo didn't answer.”** Your request went unanswered: let the host know, then “Ask Hugo” again.
- **“Lending needs KyberNow Station 0.9.0 or later.”** Update the Station.
