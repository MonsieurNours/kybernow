The KyberNow Link apps also show the PS5 or PS4 in your home: the console's picture and sound, driven by your device's controller. The console appears on the home screen, next to your computers.

## Before you start

- A PS5 or PS4, switched on or in rest mode, on the same network as your device (to play from further away, see below).
- An up-to-date KyberNow Link app: Mac, iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, Windows, Linux, Steam Deck, Raspberry Pi, Android or LG TV. The browser doesn't show consoles.
- The account you play with on the console.

## Pair the console

1. Switch the console on or leave it in rest mode. It appears on the home screen, marked “On your network”, with its name and model. If it doesn't appear, open “Add a computer or a console” › “By address” and type its address.
2. Choose “Pair”.
3. Choose “Sign in to PlayStation Network”: the app opens Sony's sign-in page. KyberNow only reads the account's ID and online ID, once for all the consoles on that account. The account is used for nothing else.
4. On the PS5, open Settings › System › Remote Play › Link Device. On a PS4, the same screen is in the Remote Play connection settings.
5. Type the “8-digit code” shown by the console into the app, then choose “Pair”. The code changes every 5 minutes: keep the console's screen open.

The app keeps the pairing. You don't do it again.

> Good to know: on a TV (Apple TV, Android TV, LG), there is no sign-in page. Receive the account from another device (see below), or choose “Enter the ID by hand”. On Linux, the sign-in page opens in your browser: once you are signed in, copy the address of the almost empty page that appears and paste it into the app.

### Several accounts

Several accounts can live on the same device, each with its own consoles, and a console can be paired with several accounts, one profile per person. Everything is managed in the “PlayStation” tab of “Add a computer or a console”. On the home screen, a chip next to “Play” picks the account for the game; the app remembers it for each console.

## Play

“Play” starts the game. With a console in rest mode, “Wake and play” wakes it first.

The game has the same bar, the same statistics and the same shortcuts as a computer session, plus “Put the console in rest mode”. On the controller, holding <kbd>Options</kbd> <kbd>Create</kbd> <kbd>L1</kbd> <kbd>R1</kbd> together opens the bar; while it is open, the console receives a controller at rest.

- The console sends 1080p at most, at 60 frames per second. On Mac, iPhone, iPad and Apple TV, the app upscales the picture to your screen's pixels, with adjustable sharpness.
- On an HDR screen, the game is in HDR (in H.265, on PS5).
- One game at a time per console.
- When the console can't stream what it is showing (some videos), the app says “The console can't stream what it's showing.”

## Controller, keyboard and mouse

A DualSense connected over USB or Bluetooth comes through in full: buttons, sticks, adaptive triggers, rumble, touchpad and gyroscope. On a Mac, plugged in over USB, it also gets HD haptics. The console settings set the strength of “Rumble” and “Adaptive triggers”, from 0 to 100%. Any other controller is seen as a PlayStation controller, with simple rumble.

Without a controller, the keyboard and mouse stand in for one:

- the keyboard on the buttons and the left stick (WASD, or ZQSD on an AZERTY keyboard);
- the mouse on the right stick, its clicks on <kbd>R2</kbd> and <kbd>L2</kbd>.

Click in the picture to play. <kbd>⌃⌥⌘M</kbd> (on Windows and Linux, <kbd>Ctrl+Alt+Shift+M</kbd>) gives the mouse back. In the console settings, the layout can be changed key by key, along with the mouse's “Sensitivity”, its “Starting response” and the “Minimum tilt” (to get past games' dead zone). “Default layout” resets everything.

When the console opens its keyboard (a search, a message), type straight on yours: “Type your text · Return to accept · Escape to cancel”.

On iPhone and iPad, the on-screen controller takes on the console's symbols (✕ ○ □ △). On iPad, the keyboard and mouse work as they do on the Mac.

## Console settings

The cog next to “Play” opens the console settings. By default, “Auto”: the best the console and your screen allow. Otherwise, the chip switches to “Custom”:

| Setting | Choices |
|---|---|
| Resolution | Auto, 1080p, 720p, 540p, 360p |
| Frame rate | 60 fps, 30 fps |
| Bitrate | Auto (follows the resolution), or from 2 to 100 Mbit/s |
| Format | Auto (H.265 if the device can play it), H.264 (no HDR), H.265 |
| Full screen | “Open the game in full screen”, remembered for each console |

The PS4 only sends H.264. Picture settings apply to the next game; keyboard, mouse and DualSense settings change straight away.

## On your other devices

A pairing works for all your devices: you don't go through the console's code again.

- **Between your Apple devices**, with iCloud on, your accounts and consoles follow by themselves, like your computers.
- **To a TV, a PC or another device**:
  1. On the device that should receive them, open the “PlayStation” tab and choose “Receive from another device”. A six-digit code appears.
  2. On the device that already has the accounts, open “Add a computer or a console” › “PlayStation” › “Send to a device”.
  3. Choose the device, type the code, then “Send”. The code expires after 5 minutes.

## Away from home

- **Through a VPN**: add the console with “By address”, using its address on the VPN. The app reaches it directly.
- **Through KyberNow's relay**: you need a Station switched on at home, on the same network as the console, with “Access away from home” turned on in Admin. The Station forwards the game without decoding it, and the app shows “Via the relay”, as it does for a computer. Nothing to set up on the console.

KyberNow never goes through Sony's servers. Without a Station switched on at home, only a VPN lets you play from afar. The relay is explained in [Reach your computer away from home](/guide/hors-de-chez-vous/).

## If something goes wrong

- **The console doesn't appear.** It must be switched on or in rest mode, on the same network. Otherwise, use “By address”.
- **“The console isn't answering: keep the ‘Link Device’ screen open.”** Go back to the console's pairing screen, then start again.
- **“Code refused: it changes every 5 minutes.”** Type the code shown right now.
- **“The console is already being played remotely by another device.”** Close Remote Play on the other device.
- **“The console no longer recognizes this device: pair it again.”** The account was removed from the console: pair it again.
- **“Couldn't read the account ID: try again, or enter it by hand.”** Sign in again, or use “Enter the ID by hand”.
