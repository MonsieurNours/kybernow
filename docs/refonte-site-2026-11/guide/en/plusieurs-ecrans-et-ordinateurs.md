With the KyberNow Link apps for Mac, Windows and Linux, a session can use every display on your device, each at its own size. You can also open several computers at the same time, one window per computer.

## Every display on the device

The computer creates a virtual display for each display on your device, laid out the same way. Each display on the device shows its own one full screen. With the desktop mouse, the pointer moves from one display to the next as usual.

1. Open the computer settings, Picture section.
2. Under “Resolution”, choose “Displays”:
   - “Auto” (the default): every display with the Desktop and Max profiles, a single one with Game and Custom;
   - “This display”: the display the app's window is on;
   - “All displays”.
3. The home screen confirms what the session will use, for example “3 displays, sized for this device's screens”.

Worth knowing:

- **Four displays at most.** Mirrored displays count as one.
- **The device's main display** becomes the computer's main display: the taskbar and full-screen games go there.
- **Refresh rate**: the main display keeps its own, the others switch to 60 Hz. A still display costs almost nothing, since the picture is only sent when the screen changes.
- **A single session bar**, on the display where the pointer is. Its controls apply to the whole session.
- **No “Full screen”** in the bar: the windows stay full screen. To work in a window, choose “This display”.
- **A display plugged in or unplugged during a session**: the session rebuilds itself with the new displays, without going back to the home screen.

“Displays” is dimmed with a custom resolution, a session in a window, or when the virtual display is turned off. Changed during a session, this setting applies to the next one; a profile chosen in the bar applies straight away.

## Several computers at once

Keep an eye on the office computer during a game on the living-room one, or work on two machines from the same Mac.

1. Open a first session, as usual.
2. In the session bar, open “Computers”: your other computers are listed with their status. Choose one. A computer that is offline wakes up, as it would from the home screen.
3. Its session opens in a window, on top, at two thirds of the screen. Its bar offers “Full screen”.

On Mac and Windows, “Connect” also stays available in the app icon's menu (the Mac's menu bar, next to the clock on Windows) and on the home screen (“Open KyberNow Link”).

### Who gets what

- The **active session** is the one in the front window. Only that one receives the keyboard, the mouse, the controllers, the clipboard and the sound.
- The others keep their picture on screen, without sound, and their computer receives nothing.
- “Keep sound” (the speaker button in the bar) keeps a session audible even when it isn't active.

### Switch from one computer to another

- Click its window.
- The “Next computer” shortcut: <kbd>⌃⌥⌘O</kbd> on Mac, <kbd>Ctrl+Alt+Shift+O</kbd> on Windows and Linux. It works in full screen too, where <kbd>⌘⇥</kbd> and <kbd>Alt+Tab</kbd> go to the computer.
- “Show” in the bar's “Computers” menu or in the icon's menu.

“Connect” on a computer already in a session brings its window to the front: the home screen then shows “Show session”. Closing a window or choosing “Disconnect” ends only that session; quitting the app ends them all.

> Good to know: only the first session can use every display. The ones after it take one display each. The living-room apps, the Steam Deck, phones, tablets, TVs and the browser stay on one computer and one display at a time.

## If something goes wrong

- **“The computer could not create this display.”** The computer refused one of the displays; the others work. Try “This display”, or a lower resolution with “4K at most”.
- **Only one display opens even though “All displays” is chosen.** The Station handles only one display, or the virtual display is turned off: update the Station.
- **“Connect” is inactive, with “Four computers at most at the same time.”** Close one of the sessions.
- **A session doesn't respond to the keyboard.** It isn't active: click its window.
