With a KyberNow account, your computers appear by themselves on each of your devices, at home and anywhere else. You can also open them to other people, each with their own rights.

The account is free and optional: at home, adding a device with a code, sharing and lending all work without it. It lives on KyberNow's relay, share.kybernow.app, which also holds everyone's rights. If you run your own relay, the account is created on yours (see [Install your own relay](/guide/installer-votre-relais/)).

## Create your account

1. Open share.kybernow.app and choose “Create an account”: your name, your email address and a password of at least 10 characters. Passwords that are too common are refused.
2. Verify your address: open the link in the email you receive, valid for 24 hours, or type the six-digit code it contains.
3. Protect your account. The “Protect your account” page suggests a passkey, which we recommend: a key kept by your phone or your computer, unlocked with your face, your fingerprint or your PIN. You can also choose two-factor authentication with an authenticator app; if you do, keep the recovery codes it gives you.
4. A welcome tour then offers to attach your computer, then to open it to the people close to you. “Skip” closes it.

The KyberNow Link app does not create accounts: its “Create an account” button opens this same page in the device's browser. Changing your password or deleting the account also happens on the site, under “Account and security”.

## Attach your computer

Attaching the computer means declaring it to your account. You do it from the computer itself, so nobody can attach someone else's computer.

- **While the Station installs**, on Windows or on a Mac, the “Meanwhile: your KyberNow account” screen offers “Sign in” or “Create an account”. The installation carries on while you sign in. “Later” leaves the attaching for Admin.
- **Afterwards, in Admin**, under KyberNow account: choose “Sign in”. The account page opens in the browser. Sign in, say who the computer belongs to (“Me”, or a family or team you manage), then “Attach”. Admin shows “Computer attached to” followed by your name.

Once the computer is attached, Admin switches on “Accept KyberNow accounts” and “Access away from home”. You can turn either of them off.

## Connect your devices

In the KyberNow Link app, choose “Connect an account”: the button is at the top of the home screen, and in the app's Settings, in the “Accounts” section.

- **On a Mac, a PC, a phone, a tablet or a Linux desktop**, the relay's page opens in a system window. Password, passkey and two-factor authentication all happen there, never in the app.
- **On the Apple TV, a TV, the Steam Deck or in the living room**, an eight-character code and a QR code appear. Scan the QR code with your phone, or open share.kybernow.app/tv and type the code, then tap “Connect”. The code renews itself after a few minutes.

The app shows “Signed in to” followed by the account's name, and your computers arrive on the home screen, with no pairing. A computer already added on this device appears only once. A badge says where each one comes from: “Account”, the name of a family or team, “Opened by Léa”, or “Paired”.

On a Windows PC, the KyberNow Link installer also offers to connect your account, on the first installation. For an account on a relay other than share.kybernow.app, choose “Another relay…”; to add a second one, “Add an account”.

## Open your computer to someone

On the account page, open “Computers”, choose the computer, then “Add a person”.

1. Type their email address. People you already know are suggested as you type.
2. Tick their rights and, if you like, an end date.
3. Confirm. They receive an email. If they already have an account, the invitation waits for them under “Invitations”, on their account page: they choose “Accept”. Otherwise, the link in the email offers to create their account.

An unanswered invitation expires after 30 days. “Resend the invitation” sends the email again and extends it for another 30 days. If the person already had access, their rights are updated. On the computer's page, “Who has access” lists everyone, and “Remove” ends someone's access.

| Right | What it allows | For example |
|---|---|---|
| “Watch” | See the computer's screen, with no keyboard, mouse or controller | Your son, who wants to show you his game |
| “Open the desktop” | Connect to the computer and use it as you do, from anywhere | Your partner, picking up their files from the laptop |
| “Borrow to play” | Play the apps you allowed in Admin, under Lending, when the computer is free | Your nephew, who plays on Wednesday afternoons |
| “Join your shares” | Join a share you open, without typing a code or waiting for your approval | A friend joining you for a two-player game |
| “Administer” | Manage access, restart and update the Station, open Admin; two-factor authentication is required | The person who looks after the studio's computers |

Lending and sharing have their own articles: [Lend your computer](/guide/preter/) and [Share your screen with KyberNow Share](/guide/partager/). Someone who joins your share with “Join your shares” appears like any other guest, and you can remove them.

## Watch

Someone with the “Watch” right sees “Watch” on the computer's card, instead of “Connect”. If they can also open the desktop, “Watch” is in the computer's menu.

They see the computer's actual screen, as it is. Nothing changes for you: no virtual display is created. They join the share that is already open as a spectator, or a share the Station opens for them and closes when they leave. With several screens, their bar offers “Screen” to switch from one to another, and “Stop watching” to leave.

You always know about it: Admin shows “Léa is watching your screen”, and the share panel lists her with “Remove”. You can also hand her the controllers or the keyboard, as with any guest.

“Watch” is refused while the computer is lent, when sharing is turned off in Admin, and with a Station that is too old: update it.

## Access links

To open the computer to someone whose address you don't have, create a link that works only once.

1. On the computer's page, open “Access links”, then “Create a link”.
2. Tick the rights, choose how long the link works (“The link works for” 1, 7 or 30 days) and, if you like, “Access until (optional)”.
3. The link is shown only once: “Copy the link”, then send it to one person.
4. They open it, sign in or create their account, then choose “Accept”. After that, the link no longer works.

As long as it hasn't been used, “Revoke” cancels it. Access already given through a link is removed under “Who has access”. A computer keeps ten pending links at most.

## Your settings follow you

For each computer on the account, the profile, the video format, the bitrate, sharp text (4:4:4), HDR and immersive mode carry over from one device to another: set them once on the Mac and you find them on the tablet. The screen, the sound, the controllers and the shortcuts stay specific to each device.

To keep separate settings on one device, turn off “Sync computer settings” in the app's Settings, in the “Accounts” section. It is on to begin with.

## Move a computer

A computer can change owner: move from you to your family or team, or come back to you.

1. On the computer's page, choose “Move…”, then the destination: you, or a family or team you manage.
2. The “What changes” preview shows who loses access, the computer groups the computer leaves and the pending links that are cancelled.
3. Leave “Keep the access given to people” ticked to keep the rights given to individuals, or untick it.
4. Confirm with “Move” followed by the computer's name.

You must manage both sides, with two-factor authentication. To take a computer out of a team, you must be one of its Owners.

## Sign out on a device

In the app's Settings, in the “Accounts” section, “Sign out” removes the account's computers from the device. The ones you added with a code stay. “Manage account” opens the account page; on a TV, a QR code opens it on your phone.

## Security

- **Your password never goes through the app.** You sign in on the relay's page.
- **Short-lived access.** To open a computer, the device presents a token signed by the relay, valid for two minutes, which the app renews by itself. The Station checks the signature every time.
- **The computer has the last word.** In Admin, under KyberNow account, with “Accept KyberNow accounts” turned off, only your paired devices and invitations get in. The “Came in with an account” list shows each person and each device; “Block” or “Block the account” holds even if the relay says yes.
- **You are told.** The first time a device opens your computer, you receive an email, and Admin records it in its feed.
- **Forgotten devices are signed out.** A device left unused for 90 days is signed out of your account. “Account and security” lists your devices, with when each one was last used, and “Disconnect” removes one. A new password signs them all out.
- **The picture stays encrypted.** Between the app and your computer, the picture, the sound and your keystrokes stay encrypted end to end, just as without an account. The relay does not read them.
- **Your data belongs to you.** “Export my data” gives you a file with everything the account holds. “Delete the account” asks for your password or passkey again, detaches your computers and signs out your devices.

## If something goes wrong

- **“Sign in” is greyed out in Admin, under KyberNow account.** First set a relay in Admin, Link devices › “Away from home”: the relay is what holds the accounts.
- **The app says the Station doesn't give tokens to KyberNow accounts.** The Station is too old: [update it](/guide/mettre-a-jour/).
- **“This computer is no longer on your account.”** Your access was removed, or the computer was detached. Ask its owner.
- **“This account was signed out of this device: sign in again.”** The device had not been used for 90 days, or the password changed. Choose “Connect an account” again.
- **The “Administer” right doesn't work.** It needs two-factor authentication or a passkey on the account, the owner's included.
- **The verification email doesn't arrive.** Look in your spam folder, then choose “Resend the email” on the “Check your email” page.
- **Forgotten password.** On the sign-in page, “Forgot your password?” sends a link valid for one hour.
