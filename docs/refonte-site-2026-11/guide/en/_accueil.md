From installing the Station to your first session, each article follows the order of the screens. Start with what you want to do.

## Get started

What you need before installing: the computer, your devices and a good network.

<!-- articles : ce-qu-il-vous-faut -->

## Install the Station

KyberNow Station goes on the computer you want to reach, in a few minutes, with its virtual displays and sound output.

<!-- articles : installer-station-windows, installer-station-mac, installer-station-linux -->

## Connect a device

On the same network, your computer shows up by itself in the app. You accept its code in Admin, and the device is added.

<!-- articles : connecter-un-appareil, link-mac, link-windows, link-linux-steam-deck, apple-tv, iphone-ipad, android, televiseur-lg, raspberry-pi, navigateur -->

## During a session

Adjust the picture and the bitrate to suit your network. Your device's keyboard, controller, stylus, microphone and camera also carry over to the computer.

<!-- articles : profils-et-qualite, clavier-et-raccourcis, stylet-micro-webcam, plusieurs-ecrans-et-ordinateurs -->

## Consoles

Your PS5 or PS4 appears next to your computers, and you play it on the nearest screen.

<!-- articles : playstation -->

## Share

Let a friend into your session with a code, or lend them the computer when you aren't using it.

<!-- articles : partager, preter -->

## Accounts and teams

With an account, your computers follow you onto every device, and open to your family or your team with precise rights.

<!-- articles : compte-kybernow, familles-et-equipes -->

## Away from home

At a hotel or at work, reach your computer through KyberNow's relay or a VPN, with nothing to open on your router.

<!-- articles : hors-de-chez-vous, installer-votre-relais -->

## Maintenance

Update the Station and your apps, or remove everything cleanly.

<!-- articles : mettre-a-jour, desinstaller -->
