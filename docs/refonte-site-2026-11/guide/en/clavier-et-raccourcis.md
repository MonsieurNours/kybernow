Your keystrokes, clicks and controller go to the computer, apart from a few shortcuts the app keeps for itself. Immersive mode sends the system's shortcuts as well.

## The mouse

In the session bar, the mouse chip shows the current mode. It offers:

- “Desktop mouse”: the pointer behaves as it does on a desktop, moving in and out of the window;
- “Game mouse”: the pointer is captured and the mouse sends its raw movements, the way shooters expect them;
- “Auto” (the default): desktop mouse, switching to game mouse when a game fills the computer's screen.

The choice applies straight away and is kept for this computer. The shortcut <kbd>⌃⌥⌘M</kbd> (Mac) or <kbd>Ctrl+Alt+Shift+M</kbd> (Windows, Linux) switches between the two.

## Immersive mode

In the Mac, Windows and Linux apps, immersive mode sends the whole keyboard to the computer, system shortcuts included: <kbd>⌘⇥</kbd>, <kbd>⌘ Space</kbd> and Mission Control on the Mac, <kbd>Alt+Tab</kbd>, the <kbd>⊞</kbd> key, <kbd>Ctrl+Esc</kbd> and <kbd>Alt+Esc</kbd> on Windows and Linux.

1. Open the computer settings (the cog), Controls section.
2. Choose “Immersive mode”:
   - “In full screen” (the default): only when the session is full screen;
   - “Always”: in a window too;
   - “Never”: the system's shortcuts stay with your device.

The change applies straight away. To leave immersive mode, leave full screen (<kbd>⌃⌥⌘F</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl+Alt+Shift+F</kbd>) or switch to another window with the mouse. The app's shortcuts stay with the app, and <kbd>Ctrl+Alt+Del</kbd> stays with the system.

On the Mac, the first time, macOS asks for the Accessibility permission for KyberNow Link. Without it, nothing changes: the setting shows “Open Settings” so you can grant it (System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility). In immersive mode, facing a PC, <kbd>⌘</kbd> becomes the <kbd>⊞</kbd> key and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> stays <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>.

## Shortcuts in the Mac, Windows and Linux apps

| Mac | Windows and Linux | Action |
|---|---|---|
| <kbd>⌃⌥⌘D</kbd> | <kbd>Ctrl+Alt+Shift+D</kbd> | Disconnect |
| <kbd>⌃⌥⌘F</kbd> | <kbd>Ctrl+Alt+Shift+F</kbd> | Full screen or window |
| <kbd>⌃⌥⌘M</kbd> | <kbd>Ctrl+Alt+Shift+M</kbd> | Desktop mouse or game mouse |
| <kbd>⌃⌥⌘S</kbd> | <kbd>Ctrl+Alt+Shift+S</kbd> | Statistics: path of a frame, latency, bitrate, losses |
| <kbd>⌃⌥⌘O</kbd> | <kbd>Ctrl+Alt+Shift+O</kbd> | Next computer, with several sessions open |
| <kbd>⌃⌥⌘R</kbd> | <kbd>Ctrl+Alt+Shift+R</kbd> | Start or stop recording |
| <kbd>⌃⌥⌘V</kbd> | <kbd>Ctrl+Alt+Shift+V</kbd> | Turn the microphone on or off |
| <kbd>⌃⌥⌘C</kbd> | <kbd>Ctrl+Alt+Shift+C</kbd> | Turn the camera on or off |

These shortcuts stay on your device and are never sent to the computer.

### On the Mac

- <kbd>⌘</kbd> + key is sent to a PC as <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + key: <kbd>⌘C</kbd> copies, <kbd>⌘V</kbd> pastes. The “⌘ key sent as Ctrl” setting changes this; it doesn't apply in immersive mode.
- The session bar opens when the pointer rests for a moment against the top edge of the picture; a short line, at the top centre, marks the spot.
- <kbd>L1</kbd> <kbd>R1</kbd> <kbd>Start</kbd> <kbd>Select</kbd>, held together, bring up the session bar from the controller.
- In full screen, the app turns on macOS Game Mode, which gives the session priority. To turn it off: the controller icon in the menu bar, “Turn Off Game Mode”.

### On Windows

- The session bar appears when the pointer touches the top of the screen.
- The bar's “Keys” menu sends what Windows keeps for itself: “Ctrl + Alt + Del”, “⊞ key alone”, “Alt + Tab”, “Print Screen”.

### On Linux

- On the desktop, the session bar appears when the pointer nears the top of the screen: latency, resolution, frames per second, decoder, profile, keys, mouse, full screen, Quit.
- <kbd>Esc</kbd> held for a second frees the mouse from a game.
- The “Keys” menu is the same as on Windows.

## Facing a Mac Station

When the computer is a Mac, a PC keyboard arrives with the <kbd>⊞</kbd> key as <kbd>⌘</kbd>.

- In the Windows, Linux, Android and LG apps and in the browser, the “Ctrl acts as ⌘” setting (Controls section, on by default) swaps the two keys: you copy and paste with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>, as on a PC, and <kbd>⊞</kbd> becomes <kbd>⌃</kbd>.
- The “Keys” menu offers the Mac's combinations: “⌘ ⇥”, “⌘ Space”, “⌃ ⌘ Q”, “⇧ ⌘ 5”, “⌥ ⌘ Esc”.
- From a Mac, an iPhone or an iPad, <kbd>⌘</kbd> is sent as <kbd>⌘</kbd>.

## By touch, on iPhone, iPad and Android

- **Keyboard**: it opens from the session bar. A tap on the picture folds it away, without clicking on the computer.
- **On-screen controller**: a button in the bar shows it, as “Full” (a whole Xbox controller) or “Simple” (left stick, D-pad, A, B, X, Y and Menu). The computer sees it as an Xbox 360 controller. Its size, opacity and haptic feedback are set in the Controls section.

## On Apple TV

| Control | Effect |
|---|---|
| <kbd>Back</kbd> | Opens the session menu: stream health, controllers, sound, remote as a mouse, controller as a mouse, text entry, disconnect |
| <kbd>L1</kbd> <kbd>R1</kbd> <kbd>Start</kbd> <kbd>Select</kbd> | Held together, open the same menu from the controller |
| Touch surface, as a mouse | Your finger moves the pointer, a click is a left click, Play/Pause a right click |

Up to four controllers (Xbox, DualSense, DualShock), each one a player on the computer, with rumble. A Bluetooth keyboard and mouse work too.

## On an Android TV

| Control | Effect |
|---|---|
| <kbd>Back</kbd> | Opens the session menu: stream health, profiles, sound, remote or controller as a mouse, text input, keys, measurements, disconnect |
| <kbd>L1</kbd> <kbd>R1</kbd> <kbd>Start</kbd> <kbd>Select</kbd> | Held together, open the same menu from the controller |
| Controller as a mouse | Left stick: the pointer. <kbd>A</kbd> click, <kbd>X</kbd> right click, right stick scrolls, <kbd>Start</kbd> the <kbd>⊞</kbd> key, <kbd>Select</kbd> Esc, <kbd>L1</kbd> and <kbd>R1</kbd> Alt + Tab |

In the menu, the controller drives the app: <kbd>A</kbd> selects, <kbd>B</kbd> closes. “Disconnect”, in red, is the last tile.

## On an LG TV

| Control | Effect |
|---|---|
| <kbd>Back</kbd> | Opens the session menu (so does a long press on Back, or the red button): stream health, profiles, Magic Remote or controller as a mouse, keys, measurements, controllers, settings, disconnect |
| <kbd>L1</kbd> <kbd>R1</kbd> <kbd>Start</kbd> <kbd>Select</kbd> | Held together, open the same menu from the controller |
| Magic Remote as a mouse | The pointer follows the remote: <kbd>OK</kbd> left click, yellow button right click, wheel scrolls |

Outside a session, the Magic Remote, an ordinary remote or the controller move around the app. “Settings”, at the top right of the home screen, opens the TV's settings; “Computer settings” opens those of the chosen computer.

## On the Steam Deck and SteamOS

| Control | Effect |
|---|---|
| <kbd>L1</kbd> <kbd>R1</kbd> <kbd>Start</kbd> <kbd>Select</kbd> | Held together, open the session menu: stream health, Resume, profiles, sound, controller as a mouse, Steam keyboard, keys, measurements, disconnect |
| Touch at the top of the screen | Opens the same menu |
| Trackpads | As a mouse, through Steam Input (“Gamepad with Mouse Trackpad” template) |
| Keyboard | In the menu, opens Steam's keyboard: the text goes to the computer |
| Controller as a mouse | Left stick: the pointer, <kbd>RT</kbd> faster, <kbd>LT</kbd> more precise. Right stick scrolls, <kbd>A</kbd> <kbd>B</kbd> <kbd>X</kbd> click |

Outside a session, with the controller: <kbd>A</kbd> confirms, <kbd>B</kbd> goes back, <kbd>Y</kbd> opens the computer settings, <kbd>X</kbd> adds a computer, <kbd>L1</kbd> and <kbd>R1</kbd> change profile, <kbd>Start</kbd> opens the app settings. Up to four controllers, each one a player on the computer, with their rumble.

## If something goes wrong

- **<kbd>⌘⇥</kbd> switches apps on the Mac instead of on the computer.** Immersive mode is waiting for the Accessibility permission, or it is set to “Never”. Check the “Immersive mode” setting.
- **The pointer is stuck in the window.** The mouse is in game mouse mode: <kbd>⌃⌥⌘M</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl+Alt+Shift+M</kbd> gives it back (on Linux, <kbd>Esc</kbd> held for a second).
- **The session bar doesn't open with the pointer.** In game mouse mode, the pointer is captured: use the mouse shortcut, the app icon's menu or the controller.
- **“Controllers unavailable on this computer.”** The computer doesn't create a controller. It still drives the app (menus, bar), but doesn't reach the computer.
