On iPhone and iPad, your computer fills the screen. You control it by touch, with the iPad's keyboard and trackpad, with Apple Pencil or with a controller.

## Before you start

- An iPhone or iPad running iOS 18 or iPadOS 18, or later.
- The Station installed on the computer you want to reach.

## Install the app

1. Open the App Store and search for “KyberNow”.
2. Install the app, then open KyberNow.
3. Allow access to the local network: without it, the app cannot find your computer.

The first time, three welcome screens offer you a KyberNow account, which is optional: “Connect an account” opens the relay's page, where you enter your password, use your passkey or complete two-factor authentication. See [Your KyberNow account](/guide/compte-kybernow/).

## Add your computer

Choose “Add a computer or a console”, then one of these ways:

- **From your Mac**: the computers added in KyberNow Link on your Mac arrive with their access, synced through iCloud. You need the same iCloud account.
- **On your network**: your computer shows up by itself. Enter the account created when the Station was installed.
- **On-screen code**: the app shows a six-digit code. Accept it in Admin, in the Link devices space (on a PC, also in the panel of the icon by the clock). Nothing to type.
- **Address**: the computer's IP address or name and Admin's port. This is the one to choose over a VPN. Then compare the fingerprint with the one in Admin.
- **Pairing QR code**: in Admin, in the Link devices space, click “Add a device”, then point the camera at the QR code. The fingerprint is checked automatically.

A `kybernow://` link received through AirDrop or Messages opens the computer's card directly, already filled in.

“With a friend account” is for [borrowing a computer](/guide/preter/), “PlayStation” for [playing your console](/guide/playstation/).

Then choose a profile (Game, Desktop, Max or Auto), and “Connect”.

## By touch

- Touch works as a trackpad or as direct touch, as you choose in the computer's settings, in the Controls section.
- “Keyboard”, in the session bar, opens the keyboard. Above it, sticky keys give you Ctrl, Alt and ⊞. A tap on the picture puts it away.
- “On-screen controller” shows an Xbox controller under your thumbs, with the “Full” or “Simple” layout. The computer sees it as an Xbox 360 controller.

## With a keyboard, trackpad, stylus or controller

- On iPad, the keyboard and trackpad work as they do on a computer.
- Apple Pencil reaches the computer as a real stylus, with pressure and tilt. See [Stylus, microphone, camera and recording](/guide/stylet-micro-webcam/).
- A Bluetooth controller (Xbox, DualSense, DualShock, MFi) pairs in the device's Bluetooth settings.

The details of the controls are in [Keyboard, mouse and shortcuts](/guide/clavier-et-raccourcis/).

> Good to know: away from home, on 4G or 5G, the app goes through the KyberNow relay. See [Reach your computer away from home](/guide/hors-de-chez-vous/).

## If something goes wrong

- **The computer does not show up.** Check that KyberNow has access to the local network, in Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network, and that the device is on the same network as the computer.
- **The camera does not read the QR code.** Allow the camera for KyberNow in Settings, or use the on-screen code.
- **“Password refused: update it in the settings.”** Enter the new password in the computer's settings, in the Station section.
- **The Mac's computers do not arrive.** Check that the Mac and the device use the same iCloud account, and open KyberNow Link on the Mac.
