## 1. Subject

The Controller uses the KyberNow service (accounts, console and public relay `share.kybernow.app`) to manage an organization: its members, its groups, rights on its computers and the access log. For this, the Processor processes personal data on behalf of the Controller. This agreement sets out the terms of that processing, in accordance with article 28 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR") and French law no. 78-17 of 6 January 1978.

The processing is described in **annex 1** (subject, duration, nature, purpose, data, data subjects).

This agreement does not cover data that the Processor processes as controller on its own account: KyberNow accounts themselves (sign-in, account security, service emails), relay security and its technical logs, described in the privacy policy.

## 2. Duration

This agreement takes effect on acceptance and lasts as long as the organization exists on the service. Obligations on the fate of the data (article 11) and confidentiality (article 4) survive its end.

## 3. The Controller's instructions

3.1. The Processor processes the data only on the Controller's **documented instructions**. These instructions are: this agreement, the terms of use, and the settings the Controller makes in the console (members, roles, groups, rights, expiry dates, attached computers, enrolment tokens).

3.2. If Union or French law requires the Processor to carry out processing that the Controller has not asked for, the Processor informs the Controller beforehand, unless that law prohibits it.

3.3. The Processor informs the Controller without delay if it considers that an instruction infringes the GDPR or another data protection rule.

## 4. Confidentiality

People authorised to process the data at the Processor have committed to confidentiality or are bound by it by law. Today, only the publisher administers the relay. `[TO UPDATE if other people gain access.]`

## 5. Security

5.1. The Processor implements the technical and organizational measures described in **annex 2**, appropriate to the risk, in accordance with GDPR article 32.

5.2. It may change them, provided the level of protection is not lowered.

## 6. Sub-processors

6.1. The Controller gives the Processor **general authorisation** to use the sub-processors listed in **annex 3**.

6.2. The Processor informs the Controller of any addition or replacement of a sub-processor, by email to the Owners and in the console, **at least 30 days** beforehand. The Controller may object during that period, on reasonable grounds relating to data protection. If no solution is found, the Controller may end the agreement and delete the organization at no cost.

6.3. The Processor imposes on each sub-processor, by contract, the same data protection obligations as in this agreement, and remains liable to the Controller for their compliance.

## 7. Transfers outside the European Union

The Processor processes the data in France and does not transfer it outside the European Union. Any future transfer will require the prior notice set out in article 6.2 and a safeguard under GDPR article 46 (for example the European Commission's standard contractual clauses).

## 8. Data subjects' rights

8.1. The Controller answers data subjects' requests. The console gives it the means to do so itself: view and remove members and their rights, read and export the log.

8.2. If a person contacts the Processor about the organization's data, the Processor forwards the request to the Controller within **5 working days** and does not answer it itself, unless instructed by the Controller.

8.3. The Processor assists the Controller, as far as possible, where the console is not enough.

## 9. Data breaches

9.1. The Processor notifies the Controller of any personal data breach affecting the organization **without undue delay and at the latest within 48 hours** of becoming aware of it, by email to the organization's Owners.

9.2. The notification states, as far as possible: the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number of people and data affected; the contact point; the likely consequences; the measures taken or proposed, including to limit its effects (for example: turning off "Accept KyberNow accounts" on the organization's Stations, revoking sessions). What is not yet known is passed on afterwards, as it becomes available.

9.3. It is for the Controller to notify the CNIL and, where applicable, the data subjects. The Processor assists it.

## 10. Assistance to the Controller

The Processor assists the Controller, by providing the information it holds, with its impact assessments (article 35), prior consultation of the CNIL (article 36) and security of processing (article 32).

## 11. Fate of the data at the end

11.1. When the organization is deleted, or at the end of this agreement, the Controller can **export** its data from the console (members, groups, rights, log) before deleting the organization.

11.2. The Processor then **erases** the organization's data **within 30 days, backups included**, unless required by law to keep it. It confirms the erasure in writing on request.

11.3. Members' personal accounts are not erased: they belong to their holders. Only their links with the organization disappear.

## 12. Documentation and audits

12.1. The Processor makes available to the Controller the information needed to demonstrate compliance with this agreement: this agreement, its annexes, the record of processing carried out on behalf of its customers, and on request a more detailed description of the security measures.

12.2. The Controller may have an **audit** carried out, by itself or by an auditor bound by confidentiality who is not a competitor of the Processor, **once a year** at most, except after a breach, with **30 days'** notice. The audit is first carried out on documents; an on-site audit does not extend to the publisher's home without consent `[TO DECIDE: the relay is currently at the publisher's home]`. Its costs are borne by the Controller.

## 13. The Controller's obligations

The Controller:

- gives the Processor instructions that comply with the GDPR and has a legal basis for the processing it entrusts to it;
- **informs the data subjects**, in particular its employees or staff: that a log keeps who opens which computer, when and from where; who reads it; for how long;
- complies with employment law where the organization is an employer: prior information of employees (article L. 1222-4 of the French Labour Code), consultation of the works council (CSE) where required (article L. 2312-38), proportionate use of the log;
- only gives rights on a computer to people who need them, and removes them when they leave;
- keeps its own record of processing.

## Annex 2: security measures

**Access and authentication**

- Passwords as Argon2id hashes, at least 10 characters, known passwords refused.
- Two-factor authentication (TOTP) and passkeys; **mandatory** for the organization's Owners and Administrators and for any account with the Administer right.
- Attempt limits: 10 per address and 5 per account in 15 minutes; identical answers whether or not an account exists.
- Console: HTTPS, `__Host-`, `Secure`, `HttpOnly` cookies, CSRF protection.

**Access to computers**

- Signed access tokens (Ed25519), valid for 5 minutes, never replayed; relay key pinned by each Station when attached; key rotation possible.
- Four safeguards on each Station: the "Accept KyberNow accounts" switch, a local list of access with blocking independent of the relay, short tokens, a notice for each new device.
- A computer can only be attached from that computer (Admin or installer); enrolment tokens limited in duration and number.
- End-to-end encrypted tunnel between the apps and the Station (Admin certificate pinned): the relay sees neither picture nor input.

**Data**

- TOTP secrets encrypted; session, email and access tokens and recovery codes kept as hashes.
- Email addresses, tokens and share codes never written to the technical logs.

- Erasure within 30 days, backups included.
